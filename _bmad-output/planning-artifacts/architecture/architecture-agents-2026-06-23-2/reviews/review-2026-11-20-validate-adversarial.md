# Adversarial divergence review · 20 November 2026

**Verdict. Changes requested.** Three high-severity findings affect posting finality, generated-content custody and Provider outcome reporting. Two medium findings affect retry admission and the public retry verdict. This standalone validation reviews the written contracts. The examples below are event orderings permitted by those contracts, with no claim of live execution.

## High findings

**H1. A delayed append can commit after Agents records abandonment.** Suppose an authorized append stalls before Conversations persists the message. At the exact posting deadline, Conversations truthfully reports the message absent. Agents records `PostingFailed`. A human abandons after a second authoritative absence read. If the original append then commits, users see the message while Agents permanently records `Abandoned`. The stable `MessageId` prevents a duplicate message but the late append is the first one. The registered existence read proves absence at its observation instant. Its contract gives the owner no obligation to fence an outstanding or delayed writer for that posting attempt. Both response modes admit this ordering. [AD-5 and the terminal-exit invariant](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L312) · [AD-6 failure predicate](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L326) · [automatic branch](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L928) · [confirmation branch](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L965) · [Conversations seam 2](../../../external-dependency-register.md#L79)

**Discuss with the Conversations owner and Architecture.** Qualify an owner observation that orders terminal absence against every earlier dispatch for that attempt, including delivery delayed until after the observation. One option is a per-attempt close-or-observe operation that fences that attempt while preserving the version's external message identity for a separately authorized retry. Keep the attempt unresolved until equivalent proof is available. Test both commit-before-close and close-before-delayed-append, followed by abandonment and restart.

**H2. The sequence returns a generated-content reference before its only legal target can exist.** The sequence identifies its recipient as Dapr Workflow. It sends that participant generated content “by reference” immediately after Provider transport. At that instant the output has yet to pass safety, and the workflow has yet to commit its generated-version mutation lease or append the protected event. AD-27 defines every `ProtectedContentReference` as a reference to a sealed event on the interaction. If safety denies the output or deletion wins the lease race, that event never exists. A team following the diagram would have to persist the unchecked output somewhere, hand Workflow a dangling reference or pass bytes across an activity boundary. [Workflow participant](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L729) · [premature return](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L875) · [safety and version append](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L896) · [AD-27 content custody](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L605)

**Autofix the diagram in an Update.** Show the generation activity holding transient output through safety, lease commitment and the version append. Only its committed version reference can return to Workflow. Exercise a crash immediately after Provider success, an output denial and a Closing winner. Inspect persisted workflow inputs and outputs for an early content reference or raw bytes.

**H3. A transport timeout can publish the wrong terminal generation outcome.** AD-13 directs a Provider error or timeout to append `GenerationFailed`. The preceding recovery rule classifies an unknowable Provider outcome as `Indeterminate`. The diagram breaks on that outcome after recording “uncertainty” and leaves the public terminal attempt fact unspecified in that branch. On a timeout after dispatch followed by an inconclusive owner lookup, one implementation can publish `GenerationFailed` and another can leave the attempt pending until monetary reconciliation. FR-12 requires a distinct terminal `Indeterminate` generation outcome. The money can stay encumbered after the attempt outcome is final. [AD-13 outcome rules](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L443) · [timeout rule](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L445) · [diagram branch](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L876) · [FR-12 public outcome](../../../prds/prd-agents-2026-06-23/prd.md#L371) · [Story 6.4 public contract](../../../epics.md#L2390)

**Autofix the branch using the approved outcome.** Qualify the failure rule with confirmed failure, and show the interaction recording `AgentGenerationOutcome.Indeterminate` exactly once when authoritative recovery cannot determine the result. Keep retry disabled and drive the reservation through its existing hold, `Unreconciled` and settlement rules separately. Test the public outcome both before the hold ends and after the final financial settlement, including replay.

## Medium findings

**M1. The allocator lacks a specified state transition for a legitimate same-attempt transport retry.** The first dispatch changes `Admitted` to `InvocationActive`. After affirmative no-use evidence AD-21 permits another transport against that same fence and reservation. AD-24 requires the `Admitted` to `InvocationActive` transition immediately before transport. The retry diagram rechecks the fence locally at Workflow and sends to the Provider without showing a call to the allocator. An allocator enforcing the single source state rejects this retry. A workflow relying on its earlier receipt can send after the allocator reclaims the expired fence between the recheck and dispatch. [AD-21 retry](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L535) · [AD-24 allocator](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L583) · [retry sequence](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L882) · [allocator profile](../../../launch-readiness-register.md#L465) · [exactly-once transition](../../../epics.md#L2202)

**Discuss with the allocator owner.** Define a linearizable authorization for a retry while the same admission remains active. Bind the new retry choice and the no-use evidence to that authorization, use the original Provider key and give the tenant no second allocation. Test the current fence, a concurrent reclaim and replay after authorization.

**M2. The shared administrative-retry verdict omits the posting-age bound.** The verdict enumerates remaining attempts, adjusted 15-minute limit, failure reason, lifecycle, suspension and lookup. It omits cumulative eligible time spent in `Approved` or `PostingFailed`. With a one-hour original lifetime, a proposal can spend 59 minutes approved, fail its first post, then spend two minutes in `PostingFailed`. Its three-attempt budget and 15-minute window still allow retry, while its posting-age bound has expired. The UI can therefore offer the retry that the command must reject. [AD-5 expiry and posting age](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L312) · [shared verdict](../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#L318) · [FR-18 explicit verdict fields](../../../prds/prd-agents-2026-06-23/prd.md#L477)

**Autofix the verdict definition.** Include the adjusted `PostingWindowElapsed` bound in the same server-side calculation used by the command and its API, BFF and UI consumers. Test opposing outcomes for the two time windows and a period when both suspension reasons overlap.

## Prior fixes and owner decisions

The revised rules preserve the original posting identity across retries, require durable approval and posting intent before append, and require matching owner evidence for success. AD-28 correctly restricts the two-second tolerance to waking the worker. Both posting branches require exact-deadline eligibility plus authoritative absence when there is no conclusive refusal. The Provider reservation now enters `Unreconciled` at the hold boundary and remains counted until audited settlement or period close. FR-28 places period close at the end of the reserving month plus the maximum configured hold. These earlier findings are closed.

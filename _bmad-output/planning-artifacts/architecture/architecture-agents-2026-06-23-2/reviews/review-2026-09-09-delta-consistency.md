# Delta Consistency Check - 2026-09-09

Scope: internal contradictions and broken references in `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md` (AD-1..AD-31, conventions, stack, seed, class diagram, capability map, prerequisites, Architecture Assumptions, Deferred) and its agreement with `launch-readiness-register.md` and `external-dependency-register.md` after the 2026-09-09 edit batch. Architecture quality is not re-reviewed; findings already in `review-2026-09-09-*.md` are listed only where the applied fix left a new contradiction. Every `[ASSUMPTION ...]` key cited inline (A-1..A-12, A-14..A-17, ARCH-A-1, ARCH-A-2, ARCH-A-4, ARCH-A-7) has a row in PRD section 8.1 or the spine table; every `EXT-*` id used in the spine has a register record; every `LR-*` gate and projection id used in the spine is in the register inventory.

## Spine-internal contradictions (fix residue)

- **AD-2 / class diagram vs AD-20 - `RestrictivenessRank`.** AD-2 says `ContentSafetyPolicy` holds "published policy versions with `RestrictivenessRank`" and the class diagram lists `RestrictivenessRank` and `ChangeKind` (no `LastLoosensVersion`); AD-20 now defines the order solely by `ChangeKind` and `LastLoosensVersion` ("B.Version >= A.Version and B.LastLoosensVersion <= A.Version") and never names a rank. Fix: replace `RestrictivenessRank` with `ChangeKind` + `LastLoosensVersion` in AD-2 and the diagram.
- **AD-13 vs sequence diagram - descriptor before reservation.** AD-13: "immediately after context measurement the interaction (1) appends one prepared descriptor ... then (2) reserves the estimated cost under `ReservationId` ... exactly one reservation per descriptor" (and AD-29 `ReservationId = H(reservation, AttemptId)`); the diagram reserves (`Workflow->>Ledger: reserve estimated cost (ReservationId)`) before safety and only appends `PrepareAttempt descriptor (AttemptId ordinal)` after `AgentCallAccepted`, so the diagram's reservation has no `AttemptId` to derive from. Fix: move the `PrepareAttempt` line to immediately before the `reserve` line and delete the post-acceptance copy.
- **AD-5 vs AD-12 - kill switch as a system-abandonment reason.** AD-5: "System abandonment carries a typed reason (`NoEligibleApprover`, `SourceConversationUnavailable`, `RemovedInConversations`, kill switch)"; AD-12: the kill switch "never system-abandons or deletes anything". Fix: drop "kill switch" from the AD-5 reason list.
- **AD-12 - two kill-switch sentences disagree on proposal creation.** "pulling it blocks ... proposal creation" vs "lets an `InvocationActive` attempt complete and record its outcome", while AD-27 makes `RecordGeneratedVersion` (which creates the proposal version in confirmation mode) the single outcome command of that activity. Fix: state that the in-flight attempt's `RecordGeneratedVersion`/`GenerationFailed` outcome is permitted (the resulting proposal then awaits under the kill-switch rules) and that "blocks proposal creation" means no new descriptor is prepared.
- **AD-4 Prevents vs AD-12 Rule - disable semantics.** AD-4 prevents "pending interactions surviving a disable or stop"; AD-12 now gives a disabled Agent kill-switch semantics under which awaiting proposals survive (rejectable, abandonable, or expiring; `Approved`/`PostingPending` complete) and are "never system-abandon[ed]". Fix: reword AD-4 Prevents to "pending interactions being approved, edited, regenerated, or invoking a Provider after a disable or stop".
- **AD-2 vs class diagram - owner of the per-Conversation block.** AD-2 places "the Agents-owned block" on `ConversationAgentState` (mirrored by the diagram's `ConversationAgentState.Block`), but the diagram also gives `TenantGovernancePolicy` a `ConversationBlocks` attribute. Fix: delete `ConversationBlocks` from `TenantGovernancePolicy` in the class diagram.
- **Class diagram vs AD-2 - owner of the kill switch.** The diagram puts `KillSwitch` on `TenantGovernancePolicy`; AD-2's `TenantGovernancePolicy` inventory (cost caps, rate limits, tenant safety restrictions, calling restriction) omits it and no AD names the aggregate that handles family `TenantKillSwitch` (AD-12 names only the family and the `agent-setup`/`launch-readiness` exposure, and groups it with `AgentSetupMutation` for locking, which points at `Agent`). Fix: add "tenant kill-switch state" to AD-2's `TenantGovernancePolicy` entry and say in AD-12 that side-effecting steps read it from `TenantGovernancePolicy` at an expected revision.
- **AD-7 - block authority contradicts itself.** "The block is set and cleared only by the Tenant Agent Administrator or the Conversation Facilitator" vs, in the same rule, "record the external removal on that aggregate ... which sets the block" and "an external removal found there sets the block". Fix: "set by the Tenant Agent Administrator, the Conversation Facilitator, or the membership step on a detected external removal, and cleared only by the two human authorities".
- **AD-2 vs AD-30 - who creates `hexa`.** AD-2: `hexa` is "created at tenant enablement under the `Platform` principal"; AD-30 enumerates the only families `Platform` may dispatch (`ProviderCatalogMutation`, platform `PolicyPublication`, `TenantProviderEnablement`, `TenantBudgetUpdate`, `TenantKillSwitch`, `DeletionRequest`) and says "every other family rejects a `Platform` principal", while `Workflow` may "never" dispatch `Agent` commands. Fix: add the tenant-enablement `Agent` creation (`AgentSetupMutation`, create-only) to the `Platform` tenant-row allowlist in AD-30, or reassign creation in AD-2.
- **AD-30 - `Platform` cannot submit the observations only it may produce.** "Platform-kind gates accept only `Platform`" and AD-17 "every public command ... declares exactly one family", yet neither AD-30's `Platform` allowlist nor the register's `OperationGateMatrix` v2 names a family for `LaunchReadinessGate` observation submission (`ReadinessInspection` is the read), and "every other family rejects a `Platform` principal". Fix: add a `ReadinessObservation` family to the register matrix (v3 row, `LR-EVENTSTORE`) and to AD-30's `Platform` allowlist (tenant `system` for `platform` scope, the target tenant for `tenant:` scope).
- **AD-20 - approval-time policy stated two ways.** "the approval command evaluates the exact version being approved ... under the then-current effective policy" vs, three sentences later, "approval time, and pre-post evaluate under the current pair when it is at least as restrictive as the snapshot pair and under the snapshot pair exactly otherwise". Fix: change the first to "under the pair selected by the restrictiveness rule below".
- **AD-7 vs AD-13 - "last pre-Provider step".** AD-7 calls membership "the last pre-Provider step of an accepted call"; AD-13 places admission (3), `ProviderInvocationAuthorized` (4), and the `InvocationActive` lease (5) after membership and before the Provider. Fix: "the last acceptance step (FR-8 step 9)".
- **AD-30 - `actor:agentsProviderAdmin` is defined inside the wrong principal.** The `Administrator` principal ("a reserved `actor:*` extension populated ... after a fresh Tenants-projection role check") is said to carry "`actor:agentsProviderAdmin` only for the `Platform` principal", but "every Agents command envelope carries exactly one principal" and `Platform` is a distinct kind. Fix: move the `actor:agentsProviderAdmin` clause into the `Platform` principal's definition.

## Spine vs register

- **AD-30 / AD-17 vs register `AuthorizedProducer` table (register lines 83-104).** AD-30: "`AuthorizedProducer` values are principal kinds: Platform-kind gates accept only `Platform`, Tenant-kind gates accept `User` holding Release Operator or `Platform`"; the register column still holds roles (`Platform Maintainer`, `Release Operator`, neither an AD-30 principal), and the three Platform-kind gates `LR-UI-CONFORMANCE`, `LR-RUNTIME-PERFORMANCE`, `LR-UI-PERFORMANCE` name `Release Operator`, which the spine rule rejects; AD-17 still says "lacking the gate's `AuthorizedProducer` role". Fix: rewrite the register column as principal kinds (`Platform` for every Platform gate; `User` (Release Operator) or `Platform` for Tenant gates) and change AD-17 "role" to "principal kind".
- **AD-10 / AD-2 / Errors row vs register Provider Readiness Contract - `EntryMissing`.** Spine: "a by-key query for a non-enabled or absent entry returns the not-found response and `EntryMissing` is reported only for the Agent's own selected entry inside `AgentReadinessStatus`"; register: "an entry not enabled for the caller's tenant, or absent, returns `EntryMissing`". Fix: amend the register sentence to "returns the not-found response; `EntryMissing` is the `ReasonCode` only inside `AgentReadinessStatus` for the Agent's own selected entry".
- **AD-29 vs register NFR-14 - `SampleId` inputs.** Spine: `SampleId = H(sample, TenantScope, QualificationSessionId, ExecutionId, SampleKind)`; register: "deterministically derived from (`QualificationSessionId`, `ExecutionId`, `SampleKind`)". Fix: add `TenantScope` and the `sample` purpose tag to the register sentence.
- **AD-17 vs register Live-Seam Matrix - `BindingStatus` vocabulary.** Spine: "`BindingStatus` (`Deferred` or `Live`)"; register: "(`Deferred`, `DeferredOutOfV1`, or `Live`)" with a `DeferredOutOfV1` row for AD-19. Fix: list all three values in AD-17.
- **AD-21 vs sequence diagram - where rate limits are enforced.** AD-21: rate limits and the concurrency maximum are enforced by "one atomic `BudgetLedger` `AdmitCall` command at FR-8 step 4"; the diagram sends "rate limits re-read" to `Interaction`.

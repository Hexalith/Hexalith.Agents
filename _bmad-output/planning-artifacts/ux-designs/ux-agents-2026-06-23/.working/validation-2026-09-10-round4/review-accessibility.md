# Accessibility Review — Hexalith Agents

Reviewer lens: accessibility as a buildable contract (WCAG 2.2 AA binding; whole-string EN/FR parity binding). Run: 2026-09-09, against the spines as edited after the FR-33 Update, the prose pass, and the `Agents.AuditOperator` revert. Inputs read in full: `DESIGN.md`, `EXPERIENCE.md`, the predecessor `.working/validation-2026-09-09-post-fr33/review-accessibility.md`, and `references/Hexalith.AI.Tools/hexalith-ux-instructions.md`. Verified against source rather than against the spine's claims: `FcAggregateDetailPage.razor(.cs)` slot and banner parameters, `FcPageHeader.razor.cs` (`HeadingId`, `HeadingTabIndex`, `FocusHeadingAsync` throw behaviour), `FrontComposerShell.razor:138-151`, every FrontComposer component carrying `role="status"`/`role="alert"`/`aria-live`, the whole `src/Hexalith.Agents.UI` tree (live-region and `Disabled` census, `aria-describedby` sites, resx key inventory), and Fluent UI Blazor v5 parameter surfaces through the MCP server for `FluentButton`, `FluentCompoundButton`, `FluentRadioGroup`, `FluentBadge`, `FluentMessageBar`, `FluentDialog`, and `FluentDialogBody`.

MCP caveat, stated once: the documentation server serves `5.0.0.26180` and the pin is `5.0.0-rc.5-26219.1`, so every v5 API fact below is "true at the served build" and re-verification is owed when the pin moves — which the spine already commits to at `DESIGN.md:214`.

## Overall verdict

The Update landed most of what it claimed, and several of its fixes are unambiguously correct: the `aria-describedby` rule at `EXPERIENCE.md:496` closes the predecessor's largest structural hole in one paragraph and binds it into `LR-UI-CONFORMANCE`; the state-slot focus rule at `EXPERIENCE.md:532` is exactly right against `FcAggregateDetailPage`'s five real slots and two banners, which I checked parameter by parameter; the `superseded by another decision` row at `EXPERIENCE.md:425` now rules for the non-actor and hands focus somewhere; `FcStatusFilterChips` is out of both grids; the resx key gap and the drift inventories are named as inventories; the 2.2.1 argument is re-founded on the essential exception and correctly disclaims the 15-minute floor as a usability commitment rather than a mechanism. Twelve of twenty-two prior findings are genuinely closed.

But the Update repeated its predecessor's signature failure twice. First, it fixed the `ConversationAgentCallPanel` critical in the seam table and the Accessibility Floor and left the `conversation-agent-call` component row — and `DESIGN.md:324` — still saying that `denied`, `generation failed`, `capacity queued` and `Posted` "each announce through those nodes", the panel's own nodes, in the same sentence that closes the panel. A story developer builds the component row. The critical is two-thirds closed and therefore still open. Second, having correctly established that `DisabledFocusable` does not exist on `FluentRadio`, it replaced the rule with one that opens a modal confirmation on every arrow keypress in a radiogroup while the editor is dirty — the precise defect the Update's own generalisation ("a confirmation is never opened by a selection event") was written to close, now readmitted as a named exception, on a native `FluentRadioGroup` whose selection has already moved by the time the handler runs. That is a worse defect than the one it replaced, and the spine declares it as the fix.

Underneath both sits an unfinished census. The politeness table is now the spine's central accessibility artifact and it asserts its own completeness — "Any event this spine names and this table omits is a defect in the table, not a deliberate silence." Four of the seven display-only outcomes the sentence directly above it enumerates are missing from it. So is the terminal success of eight of the ten confirmation families, five severe Agent-call rejections including `kill switch active` and `no eligible approver`, and the audit form's own `not available` outcome that `EXPERIENCE.md:99` says the polite node announces. The four operations the Update newly promoted to controls — the kill switch, the per-Conversation block and re-admit, administrative abandon and administrative retry — are each called a `high-impact-confirmation` family while § Confirmation contents by family remains closed at ten and carries a row for none of them, so four justification-gated dialogs have no specified contents and, since "the rendered set is what the audit record carries", no specified audit record either. And the two drift inventories the Update rewrote to fix a counting error both still miscount: the live-region row says 15 sites across 13 files where the tree holds 29 across 20 and omits `ProviderCatalog.razor` entirely; the `Disabled` row says eleven pages, enumerates four, and omits `ProposalRegenerator.razor` — the one control the predecessor's original finding named.

Two critical, ten high, seven medium, three low.

## Findings

### Live regions and announcement ownership

- **[critical]** The predecessor's critical is closed in two of the three places that state it, and the place a developer builds from still describes the destroyed nodes. `EXPERIENCE.md:121` now gives `EXT-CONV-UI-1` a third artifact kind and a focus-return contract; `EXPERIENCE.md:124` and `EXPERIENCE.md:515` assign every post-submit outcome to the persistent region. But the `conversation-agent-call` row at `EXPERIENCE.md:212` is untouched: "The panel owns one polite `role="status"` node and one `role="alert"` node … placed after Submit in DOM order. On Submit the dialog closes … `submitted`, `authoritative pending`, `denied`, `capacity queued`, `generation failed`, `PostingPending`, and `Posted` each announce through those nodes." Seven announcements, into the panel's nodes, after the sentence closes the panel. `DESIGN.md:324` matches it: "Status renders as `FluentBadge` rows: `submitted`, `authoritative pending`, and the terminal outcome" — inside the dialog, with no persistent region anywhere in the DESIGN component. Component Patterns and § Components are the two rows Story 6.7 is written from; the seam table is a dependency register entry. The defect ships. *Fix:* rewrite the `conversation-agent-call` row so the panel owns nothing after Submit — it announces only in-dialog validation and `submitted`, and every subsequent state is the contributed region's — and rewrite `DESIGN.md § conversation-agent-call` to render the status `FluentBadge` rows in the persistent region beside **Call hexa**, not in the dialog body.

- **[critical]** The version-selection fix reopens the arrow-key-opens-a-modal defect on a native radiogroup, and the mechanism it relies on does not exist. `EXPERIENCE.md:211` is correct that `DisabledFocusable` is on `FluentButton` and `FluentCompoundButton` only — I confirmed `FluentRadioGroup`'s surface is `Disabled`, `RadioDisabled`, `ReadOnly`, `Value`, `Items`, `RadioLabel`, `RadioValue` and layout properties, with no focusable-disabled anything. But the replacement is: "changing the selected version while the editor is dirty opens the unsaved-changes confirmation offering Save, Discard, or Cancel, and the selection moves only after Save or Discard resolves. This keeps the native radiogroup keyboard contract — where the arrow key *is* the selection — intact." It does the opposite. In a native radiogroup arrow keys move focus *and* selection continuously; an Approver with a dirty editor arrowing from version 1 to version 4 fires the confirmation three times, each one yanking focus into a focus-trapped dialog, and "the selection moves only after Save or Discard resolves" requires reverting a selection the browser has already committed — the suppression of native selection semantics the predecessor identified as the hazard. `EXPERIENCE.md:205` states the general rule this violates ("the confirmation is always opened by an explicit command control, never by a selection event") and `EXPERIENCE.md:222` restates it for `high-impact-confirmation`; naming this the "one exception" does not make it operable, and it is a WCAG 3.2.2 On Input failure besides. The dialog also has no contract: it carries no family, so `high-impact-confirmation`'s focus trap, `AutoFocus` Cancel, explicit `Esc` wiring and focus-return rules do not reach it, and nothing says where focus lands after Cancel or what the radio shows. *Fix:* adopt the same shape that already fixed `response-mode-toggle`. The radio group edits a **draft selection**; an explicit **View version** command control applies it, and only that control opens the unsaved-changes confirmation. Alternatively bind `FluentRadioGroup.ReadOnly` while dirty with the reason on the group via `aria-describedby` — `ReadOnly` exists at the pin and, unlike `Disabled`, keeps the group focusable. Either way, give the unsaved-changes dialog an explicit contract row rather than leaving it family-less and unspecified.

- **[high]** The politeness table omits four of the seven outcomes the paragraph above it says are all announced, and the spine tells you that is a defect. `EXPERIENCE.md:517`: "Every **display-only outcome** listed in § Proposal lifecycle — `safety blocked at approval`, `stale proposal`, `regeneration ceiling reached`, `duplicate submission (idempotent)`, `expired at approval`, `superseded by another decision`, `authority unresolved` — is announced by the node its severity selects … Any event this spine names and this table omits is a defect in the table, not a deliberate silence." The table at `EXPERIENCE.md:523-524` carries `superseded by another decision` and, ambiguously, `expired` and `safety blocked` (which read as the Agent-call states of those names, not the at-approval outcomes). `stale proposal`, `regeneration ceiling reached`, `duplicate submission (idempotent)` and `authority unresolved` appear nowhere in it. All four are exactly the case the same paragraph forbids: `EXPERIENCE.md:431-432` and `EXPERIENCE.md:426` carry them on an adjacent `FluentBadge` whose text changes, beside a control that becomes `aria-disabled` without its label changing, and `EXPERIENCE.md:510` bans that badge from being a live region. `authority unresolved` in particular fires when Tenants or Conversations drops mid-session and silently disables every resolution control on the page. *Fix:* add the four rows explicitly, and disambiguate the two existing rows by writing `expired at approval` and `safety blocked at approval` as their own entries distinct from the call states.

- **[high]** No confirmation family except the two proposal ones has an announced terminal outcome. The table's `role="status"` row carries `approved`, `posting pending`, `posted`, `proposal created`, `regenerated` — every one of them `ProposalResolution` or `ConversationPosting`. Nothing announces the `ProjectionConfirmed` success of `PolicyPublication`, `TenantBudgetUpdate` (cap or override), `AgentActivation`, `AgentSetupMutation`, `ProviderCatalogMutation`, `LegalHold`, `ExportRequest`, `DeletionRequest`, the kill switch, or the per-Conversation block and re-admit. The § High-risk pending commands sequence (`EXPERIENCE.md:446`) announces `Submitted` and `AuthoritativePending` and then goes quiet: on success the activated control loses its pending badge, focus moves per the deterministic focus rule to a status cell, and nothing says the operation succeeded. Priya publishing a safety policy or pulling a tenant kill switch — the two highest-consequence writes in the product — is told the command was accepted and never told it completed. Five severe **Agent call** outcomes are missing on the same grounds: `no eligible approver`, `kill switch active`, `conversation blocked`, `budget blocked`, `unknown outcome`, each `{colors.status-severe}` and each a terminal rejection of the product's only V1 invocation path. So is the audit form's own outcome, which `EXPERIENCE.md:99` says "the polite node" announces. *Fix:* add one generic row — every family's `ProjectionConfirmed` result announces in the polite node as a localized whole string naming the operation and the resource — plus the five call rejections (assertive, they are failures) and the audit-form `not available` outcome.

- **[high]** Two Agents-owned polite nodes and two assertive nodes co-exist on the Conversation surface for the whole time the dialog is open. `EXPERIENCE.md:510` states the rule: "Agents owns one node of each politeness per route." `EXPERIENCE.md:212` gives the panel a pair; `EXPERIENCE.md:121` and `:124` give the Conversation view a persistent contributed pair. `EXPERIENCE.md:515` closes only the other collision — "Where the panel is hosted on an Agents route — only the `/agents/conversation-call` harness … the panel suppresses its own pair" — and says nothing about the case that actually ships, where the region exists *because* the panel's nodes die. While the dialog is open the surface holds both, and the panel's assertive node is the one that fires for an in-dialog validation failure while the region's assertive node is live for the same politeness. *Fix:* say which pair speaks while the dialog is open. The cleanest reading of `EXPERIENCE.md:124` ("The panel announces only what happens while it is open") is that the panel should own **no** nodes and push its in-dialog text into the contributed region, which is mounted throughout; then there is exactly one pair on the surface and the DOM-order sentence at `:212` disappears with it.

- **[high]** The shipped live-region inventory is wrong in both directions and omits a file the predecessor named. `EXPERIENCE.md:728` claims "15 sites across 13 files"; the tree holds **29 sites across 20 files**. The row's own enumeration lists 19 files and, counting the sites it names, at least 21 — so the header contradicts its own body before it contradicts the code. The missing file is `Components/Pages/ProviderCatalog.razor`, which carries three `aria-live="polite"` regions (lines 42, 48, 54) and which the predecessor's report named explicitly. The panels also carry two sites each, not one (`OperationalStatusPanel`, `AuditEvidencePanel`, `LaunchReadinessPanel` each pair `role="status"` with `aria-live="polite"`), as do `AgentSurfaceState` and `ProposalTransitionAnnouncer`. A story developer working Story 8.6 from this row leaves the Provider catalog announcing three ways. *Fix:* replace the count with the verifiable predicate the row already half-states — "every `role="status"`, `role="alert"` or `aria-live` occurrence in `src/Hexalith.Agents.UI` outside `AgentsPageStatusRegion`; 29 occurrences in 20 files at the 2026-09-09 tree" — and add `ProviderCatalog.razor` to the enumeration.

- **[medium]** The closed speaker list is closed against the components the predecessor happened to name, not against the shell. `EXPERIENCE.md:512` ends "No other FrontComposer component carrying `role="status"`, `role="alert"`, or `aria-live` is used on an Agents route without being silenced." Verified in `FrontComposerShell.razor`: `FcDensityAnnouncer` (line 138, a visually-hidden `role="status" aria-live="polite"` density announcer) and, **inside the `main` landmark**, `FcProjectionConnectionStatus` and `FcPendingCommandSummary` (lines 149-150) render on every Agents route with no Agents-side parameter to silence them; `FcCommandPalette` carries a fourth. Three consequences. `FcProjectionConnectionStatus` is permitted by the next bullet (`:514`) but is not in the closed list, so the two sentences disagree. `FcPendingCommandSummary` is declared "out for V1" at `EXPERIENCE.md:348` and `:587`, which reads as absence but is only non-use: it is shell-rendered and speaks whenever the shell's Fluxor pending-command state is non-empty, which is precisely the moment `AgentsPageStatusRegion` is announcing `Submitted`. And `FcDensityAnnouncer` speaks on a shell setting change with no Agents involvement at all. *Fix:* name all four as shell-owned permitted speakers Agents does not duplicate and cannot silence, and state for `FcPendingCommandSummary` that Agents dispatches no shell pending-command action, so the node stays empty — which is the real mechanism behind "out for V1".

- **[medium]** Most announced strings still have no key, and the parity gate cannot see them. `AgentsResourcesParityTests` enforces parity of keys that exist (`EXPERIENCE.md:188`), so a hard-coded English announcement passes. The Update added `Showing version {n}.`, `{count} proposals changed. Refresh list.` and `Response mode unchanged: {mode}.` to the Voice and Tone table — good — but the table still supplies no string for `Version {n} generated`, `awaiting projection`, `catching up`, `pending in another session`, `connection restored`, `draft saved`, `draft discarded`, the nearing-expiry warning, or the domain consequence of connection loss. All nine are rows in the politeness table, and for a screen-reader user the announced string is the only UI those states have. *Fix:* state that every event in the politeness table has a named `Agents.<Surface>.<Item>` key, and add the nine rows with their French forms.

### Blocked-state reasons, focus, and the newly added controls

- **[high]** Save and Discard return focus to a control that has just unrendered. `EXPERIENCE.md:244`: "Both render only while the editor is dirty, both are announced in the politeness table, and after either one focus returns to the control that was activated." Save clears the dirty state by definition, and Discard does too; the sentence's first clause removes both controls from the rail at the exact commit its last clause focuses one of them. Focus falls to `document.body` on the two controls the Update added specifically to close a dead end. *Fix:* send focus to Approve, which is the control the dirty state was blocking and which becomes activatable at the same commit, and announce `draft saved` / `draft discarded` in the polite node as the table already requires. Alternatively keep Save and Discard rendered and `DisabledFocusable` with a "no unsaved changes" reason when clean, which is more consistent with the spine's own preference for focusable-disabled over absence.

- **[high]** Four operations are declared `high-impact-confirmation` families and the closed ten-family table has a row for none of them. `EXPERIENCE.md:111` makes the per-tenant kill switch and the per-Conversation removal/block/re-admit "both `high-impact-confirmation` families with a required justification"; `EXPERIENCE.md:302` and `:308` repeat it; `EXPERIENCE.md:251` adds `administrative abandon` and `administrative retry`, "each `Agents.Administrator`, audited, justification required". § Confirmation contents by family enumerates exactly ten families and carries none of the four. Because `EXPERIENCE.md:258` says "the rendered set is what the audit record carries", four justification-gated dialogs have unspecified body content and unspecified audit records. It matters to this lens twice over: the confirmation body *is* the accessible content of a focus-trapped dialog a user cannot leave without deciding, and the gated-Confirm rule at `EXPERIENCE.md:320` binds `aria-describedby` to a per-family reason string that does not exist for these four. `administrative retry` is the sharpest case: `EXPERIENCE.md:254` puts `retry posting` under family `ConversationPosting` precisely because that family's gate set requires `LR-CONVERSATIONS-MEMBERSHIP-POSTING`, while `:251` says administrative retry does **not** require Conversation read access — so it cannot be that family and has no other. *Fix:* either add four rows to the family table (and correct "the ten families" to fourteen at `EXPERIENCE.md:222`, `:442`, `:480`, `:496`), or state explicitly which existing family each maps to and what its additional required contents are.

- **[high]** The Security approval stage has no state, no announcement, and the pending contract actively mislabels it. `EXPERIENCE.md:215` makes platform safety-policy publication carry "a rendered **Security approval stage** — a second, separately authorized acceptance stage in the `PolicyPublication` pending sequence, never a checkbox the publisher ticks." Nothing else in the spine acknowledges it. The publisher activates Publish; § High-risk pending commands takes over: the control becomes `DisabledFocusable`, shows `Submitted`, then `AuthoritativePending`, and after the 8-second catch-up budget renders **`awaiting projection` with a refresh action** (`EXPERIENCE.md:446`, `:450`). The write is not awaiting a projection — it is awaiting a different human being, possibly for days — and the state token, the badge text, the announcement and the offered recovery are all wrong. Contrast `EXPERIENCE.md:320`, which gets the structurally identical deletion case right: "Deletion is `DisabledFocusable` while that approval is absent." *Fix:* give the Security approval stage a UX state alongside `AuthoritativePending` — awaiting security approval, with its own localized label, its own polite announcement, and a rendered statement of who must act — and exempt it from catch-up exhaustion so it never degrades to `awaiting projection`.

- **[high]** The deterministic focus rule emits invalid ARIA on both interactive grids. `EXPERIENCE.md:447` gives the focus target "`role="group"`, `tabindex="-1"`, and an accessible name … `role="group"` is required because ARIA prohibits a name on `role="generic"`." The rationale is right for a bare `div`, and wrong for the target it is applied to. The target is "the same item's **status cell**" in a `FluentDataGrid`, whose rows carry `role="row"`; a `role="row"` may own only `cell`, `gridcell`, `columnheader` or `rowheader`, so overriding the cell to `group` both invalidates the row's owned-element contract and strips the cell out of the grid's row/column reading, breaking grid navigation announcement at the moment of a resolution. It is also unnecessary there: `gridcell` is a name-from-author role, so the cell can carry `aria-label` as it stands. *Fix:* scope the rule to where the rationale applies — on a grid the target is the status **gridcell** with `tabindex="-1"` and `aria-label`, role untouched; on a detail route, where the target is a badge container with no implicit role, wrap it in `role="group"` (or name it from a visually-hidden text node via `aria-labelledby`).

- **[high]** Shipped code removes Approve and the Agent-call action entirely at constrained viewports, and the drift table does not record it. `Components/Shared/ProposalApprover.razor:11-31` and `AgentCallStatusFeedback.razor:30-42` branch on a `Constrained` parameter and replace the action with a `<p tabindex="0">` carrying `Agents.ProposalApprover.ConstrainedUnavailable`, commented as UX-DR40. That is the exact behaviour `DESIGN.md:382` names as the Don't ("Lock out approval at narrow widths or high zoom"), that `EXPERIENCE.md:551` forbids ("every route stays operable at 320 CSS px width (400% zoom), including proposal detail"), and that `EXPERIENCE.md:574` permits only "when its required decision context cannot render" — which a viewport width is not. It is a 1.4.10 failure on the product's highest-consequence action, it is in the tree today, and § Shipped-code corrections has no row for it; the `Disabled` row does not reach it, because the control is absent rather than disabled. The same two files also carry a self-referential `aria-describedby` pointing at the paragraph's own `id`, which describes nothing. *Fix:* add a drift row — "the `Constrained` viewport branch in `ProposalApprover` and `AgentCallStatusFeedback` removes the primary action; the action stays present and operable at 320 CSS px per § Other criteria, reflow" — with 7.3 and 6.7 as absorbing stories, and name the self-referential `aria-describedby` in the same row.

- **[medium]** The `Disabled` drift row miscounts and omits the control the original finding was about. `EXPERIENCE.md:729` says "across eleven pages", then enumerates four files and fourteen sites. The tree holds **15 `Disabled=` sites across 5 files**; the missing one is `Components/Shared/ProposalRegenerator.razor:25`, `Disabled="@_submitting"` — the regenerate-while-a-generation-is-in-flight case that `EXPERIENCE.md:298` explicitly requires to be `DisabledFocusable` with a local pending badge, and the single control the predecessor's pre-Update finding named. *Fix:* correct the count to five files / fifteen sites and add `ProposalRegenerator.razor`, with 7.3 in the absorbing-story list.

- **[medium]** The `aria-describedby` rule names no v5 binding, while every neighbouring rule does. `EXPERIENCE.md:497` is scrupulous about binding `aria-disabled` to `FluentButton.DisabledFocusable`, and `DESIGN.md:385` makes naming the v5 binding a Do. `EXPERIENCE.md:496` then requires `aria-describedby` on the same controls and names no parameter. At the served build no Fluent component in the relevant set exposes one: `FluentButton`, `FluentCompoundButton`, `FluentBadge`, `FluentRadioGroup` and `FluentMessageBar` have no `AriaDescribedBy`, and the documentation exposes no `AdditionalAttributes` splat I could confirm — `FluentRadioGroup` has `AriaLabel` only, and `FluentBadge`'s only ARIA hook is `IconLabel`. If unmatched-attribute capture is inherited from `FluentComponentBase` the rule is buildable as a plain `aria-describedby="@id"` attribute and one sentence closes it; if it is not, the whole rule needs a different mechanism and `LR-UI-CONFORMANCE` is asserting something no control can satisfy. This is the spine's most load-bearing new accessibility rule and it is the one rule with no verified binding. *Fix:* verify attribute splatting on `FluentButton` at the pin and state the binding in `EXPERIENCE.md:496` the way `:497` states `DisabledFocusable`; if splatting is unavailable, specify a wrapper that carries the description and reference it from the control.

- **[medium]** `Esc`-confirms-before-discarding requires a dialog opened from a dialog, which Fluent v5 tells you not to do. `EXPERIENCE.md:499` covers "any non-empty text input inside a dialog, including the required **Call hexa** prompt, which confirms before discarding" — and every justification-gated `high-impact-confirmation` contains a required justification `FluentTextArea` (`DESIGN.md:348`), so the rule reaches ten families' dialogs too. Fluent v5's dialog guidance is explicit: "Don't open a `FluentDialog` from a `FluentDialog`." Nesting a modal inside a modal also breaks the outer trap's inertness and leaves focus return undefined between two traps. *Fix:* replace the nested dialog with a non-modal in-dialog step — an inline confirm strip inside the same `FluentDialogBody`, focus moved to it, `Esc` again cancelling that strip — or take the second option the sentence already offers and make keep-for-session the only behaviour for text inside a dialog, with no second dialog at all.

- **[medium]** The lower-only cost input contradicts the Forms validation-timing rule. `EXPERIENCE.md:216`: "the input rejects any value above the current one with a typed localized reason." `EXPERIENCE.md:544`: "Validation runs on submit. After the first submit, a field re-validates on blur; before it, typing never produces an error." A `FluentNumberInput` that "rejects" as you type either produces an error before the first submit or silently clamps the value — the second being worse, since a screen-reader user gets no indication their entry was altered. *Fix:* state that the tenant lower-only bound is a `Max` on the input plus a submit-time validation error carrying the current boundary, per the Forms rules, and that no value is silently rewritten.

### Timing, forms, and cross-document consistency

- **[high]** A second content-imposed time limit exists and 2.2.1 is never claimed for it. `EXPERIENCE.md:254` and `:414` bound retry posting at "**3 attempts within 15 minutes of the first failure** (A-7, Architecture-owned, not tenant-configurable)", and "window expiry is a distinct exhaustion reason from attempt exhaustion". That is a time limit set by the content within which the user must act, so 2.2.1 applies to it exactly as it applies to proposal expiry — and none of the four allowances is met: it cannot be turned off, it is explicitly not configurable so Adjust is unavailable, there is no extension, and unlike expiry there is no warning at all, because the remaining time is rendered only *inside* the retry confirmation (`EXPERIENCE.md:263`). An Approver sitting on a `PostingFailed` detail page loses the retry exit at 15 minutes with no nearing-window chip, no announcement (the politeness table has no window-expiry event), and a silently mutating badge. The essential argument at `EXPERIENCE.md:559` is well made and covers only expiry. *Fix:* extend the 2.2.1 bullet to the A-7 retry window with its own essential argument (the deterministic `MessageId` and the pre-post re-validation set are what make the bound essential), add a nearing-window rendering flag and its announcement on the same terms as nearing expiry, and record the window-expiry outcome in the politeness table.

- **[medium]** The nearing-expiry chip's strings are cross-referenced to a table that does not contain them. `DESIGN.md:292` now correctly requires visible text — "`Expires in {duration}`, culture-formatted, with `Warning16` decorative beside it" — and ends "The announced string is worded separately from the chip and both live in `EXPERIENCE.md § Voice and Tone`." Neither is in that table (`EXPERIENCE.md:141-167`), which has no expiry-warning row at all, and neither key exists in the resx; the shipped tree carries `Agents.ProposalQueue.Expiry.ExpiringSoon` = "Expiring soon", a third wording with no duration. So the one status the Update fixed for no-colour-only still has no string, in either language, and the fix reads as complete because it points somewhere. *Fix:* add both rows to Voice and Tone with their French forms (`Expire dans {duration}` for the chip; a distinct announcement wording), and record the `ExpiringSoon` divergence in § Shipped-code corrections.

- **[medium]** The audit id-entry form's visible label is mandated with two different contents in the two spines. `EXPERIENCE.md:99` accepts "an interaction, proposal, governance-operation, Conversation, or inspection-case reference" and requires "a visible label naming the **five** accepted reference kinds". `DESIGN.md:244` still says "it takes an interaction, proposal, or governance-operation reference" — three. The label is the entire accessible contract of the surface, and the Update expanded the accepted set in one spine only. *Fix:* correct `DESIGN.md:244` to the five kinds; while there, note that a single input accepting five heterogeneous reference formats needs its `aria-describedby` format hint to state how the kinds are distinguished, which neither spine says.

- **[low]** The deterministic focus ladder's middle rung does not exist where most resolutions happen. `EXPERIENCE.md:447` orders the fallback "the same item's status cell or badge container … then the next row; then the page heading." Every `ProposalResolution` and `ConversationPosting` resolution is submitted from `/agents/proposals/{id}`, a Constrained detail route with no rows. *Fix:* say the ladder is status cell, then next row **on a grid**, then the page heading, so the detail case reads as a two-step rule rather than one with an unreachable middle.

- **[low]** The politeness table's `expired` row is scoped to a rendering condition that the queue's own aggregate rule already covers, and the two read as if they could both fire. `EXPERIENCE.md:523` puts `expired` in `role="alert"` "(proposal detail only, or on the queue when the expiring row is the focused one)", and `EXPERIENCE.md:526` says the queue aggregate is "never as `role="alert"` unless the row that expired is the focused one". They agree, but a developer reading only the table will not know the aggregate exists. *Fix:* one cross-reference in the table cell to § Announcement volume.

- **[low]** `FluentDialogBody.FixedHeaderFooter` already defaults to `true` at the pin, so `EXPERIENCE.md:222`, `DESIGN.md:364` and `EXPERIENCE.md:569` are specifying a default. ## What is genuinely closed

- The `aria-describedby` obligation. `EXPERIENCE.md:496` states it as a rule, gives the reason ("a focusable disabled button whose reason a screen-reader user cannot reach from the control is the same dead end as an unfocusable one"), enumerates the controls it binds, and makes it an `LR-UI-CONFORMANCE` assertion and an AT-matrix check. Only its v5 binding is unnamed.
- The detail-route heading and focus contract, end to end. Every one of those checked against `FcAggregateDetailPage.razor(.cs)`, `FcPageHeader.razor.cs` and `FrontComposerShell.razor`.

## Overall verdict

Mechanically this is the cleanest state the pair has been in: the 22 component names are identical *and identically ordered* across the DESIGN frontmatter, the DESIGN `###` sections, and the EXPERIENCE Component Patterns rows (diff-verified, the three-way parity rule holds); all 19 distinct `{path.to.token}` references resolve; all 36 source paths resolve; every internal `§` cross-reference resolves; the `FcFluentIcons` inventory now matches its source line for line; and 13 of the predecessor's 15 findings are verifiably closed. What breaks the run is not mechanics but source freshness. Both spines declare themselves "reconciled to the PRD of 2026-09-08"; `prd.md`, which both list as a source, now carries a 2026-09-09 reconciliation whose centrepiece is **FR-33, the authoritative six-role authorization matrix for every authorization rule in the PRD**. Alongside it, FR-18's approval-freezes-expiry rule, FR-18's four exits from an exhausted `PostingFailed`, FR-7's retirement of the `Caller` policy source, FR-28's per-tenant kill switch, and OQ-16's inversion of removal ownership are all missing or contradicted. Verdicts: component coverage, token completeness, visual reference, and shape fit **strong**; flow coverage, state coverage, and bloat **adequate**; inheritance discipline **thin**.

### Findings

- **high** PRD FR-28's **per-tenant kill switch** has no surface, no state, no copy, and no flow. FR-28 (`prd.md:625`) specifies precise UI-visible semantics: it "disables Agent Calls tenant-wide, lets proposals awaiting a decision be rejected or abandoned but not approved, lets `Approved` and `PostingPending` proposals complete or fail on their own terms, and deletes no proposal or Audit Evidence", is pulled by Platform Operator or Release Operator, and has three named trigger conditions (A-17). Grep for "kill switch" across both spines returns zero. The `proposal-editor` action rail (`EXPERIENCE.md:214–220`) has no kill-switch column, § Agent call (`295–317`) has no kill-switch block state, and `launch-readiness-panel` / `operational-status-panel` render no switch state. FR-18 also names "kill switch" as one of four typed system-abandon reasons. *Fix:* add a kill-switch state and control to the Launch readiness or Operational status surface (it is a Release-Operator gesture, so `Agents.Operator` is the natural home), give it a `high-impact-confirmation` family with its required contents, add a `kill switch active` blocked-call state to § Agent call, and add the "reject or abandon but never approve" constraint as a row in § Proposal editor action rail.
- **high** OQ-16 as amended 2026-09-09 **inverts the ownership the seam section pins**. The spine says "Removal of `hexa` is a Conversation-owned action. Agents reacts by blocking further calls" (`EXPERIENCE.md:104`). OQ-16 (`prd.md:869`) now says removal "is an **Agents-owned per-Conversation block**, mirrored to the Conversations participant list, **set and cleared** by the Tenant Agent Administrator or Conversation Facilitator (A-9); while set, calls are rejected pre-Provider with a typed reason, no join is re-established". So the block is an Agents-owned piece of state with an Agents-side set/clear (re-admit) authority, and the spine has no surface, control, state, confirmation family, or copy for any of it. OQ-16 also specifies a "three-part membership step that detects an external removal and sets the block before it would ever re-add", which the spine's one-line join rule (`103`) does not carry. *Fix:* add a per-Conversation block state and a set/clear control (Operational status is the likely home given its per-Conversation `safety blocked` history rows), name its confirmation family, state the disclosure treatment for the Conversation reference on the same terms as the safety-history row, and correct the seam table's ownership split.
- **medium** FR-7's call-time **`NoEligibleApprover`** rejection has no state. FR-7 (`prd.md:230–238`) makes it a typed, pre-Provider call rejection counted under FR-25 and SM-C4 — "`hexa` is unavailable, with a typed `NoEligibleApprover` rejection, in a Confirmation Response Mode Conversation whose Participants yield no Eligible Approver". § Agent call's 17 rows (`EXPERIENCE.md:295–317`) have no such row; `denied` is authorization-only, and the `operational-status-panel` per-tenant blocked counts (`191`) list context, safety, cost cap, rate limit, and capacity but not this. Related: FR-7's **configuration-time** SoD predicate is exact — reject "a policy that names no Conversation-dependent source … and fewer than two predefined Parties", and "a Facilitator-only policy is valid" — while `approver-policy-builder` states only the generic "a configuration that cannot satisfy segregation of duties" (`178`), leaving the validation rule and its error copy for a story-dev to invent. *Fix:* add the `no eligible approver` call state with its recovery (fix the Approver Policy, not retry), add it to the blocked-count list, and carry FR-7's two-part configuration predicate verbatim into the builder row.

### Findings

- **high** **Approval freezes expiry is not carried, and the spine's rule as written contradicts it.** FR-18 (`prd.md:394`) and OQ-3 (`856`) both state that "`ExpiresAt` applies only while a proposal is awaiting a decision" and "approval freezes expiry: `Approved`, `PostingPending`, and `PostingFailed` proposals never expire". The spine's `Expired` row says "Rendered whenever `ExpiresAt` has passed on any read, regardless of timer delivery" (`EXPERIENCE.md:334`) with no state carve-out — the "regardless of timer delivery" half is right, the "on any read" half is wrong for three states. Implemented literally, an `Approved` proposal whose window lapses during a slow post renders `Expired` and loses its retry-posting rail. *Fix:* add "while `Pending`, `Edited`, or `Regenerated`" to the `Expired` row and state the freeze once beside it; the `Approved` and `PostingPending` rows should say expiry no longer applies.
- **high** **The exits from an exhausted `PostingFailed` contradict FR-18.** The action rail says "`PostingFailed`, retries exhausted | `Start a new Agent Call` only" (`EXPERIENCE.md:219`), and § Proposal lifecycle repeats "once retry is exhausted the sole action is a new Agent Call" (`331`). FR-18 (`prd.md:396`) gives four exits and says the proposal *remains* `PostingFailed* until one of them: an Eligible Approver abandons it; the Tenant Agent Administrator abandons it under the audited FR-33 row **that needs no Conversation read access**; an audited administrative retry succeeds; or the system abandons it under FR-7. FR-18 states this explicitly so that "a `PostingFailed` proposal therefore always has an exit and always reaches the §9 retention clock" — under the spine's rule it has none, and nothing ever reaches retention. *Fix:* replace the row with the four exits, add the administrative abandon and administrative retry as actions with their authorizing role and confirmation family, and state that they do not require Conversation read access.
- **medium** **Four of the 13 routes have no page-component binding and therefore no state-slot mapping.** § Accessibility Floor binds heading/focus behavior to `FcAggregateListPage` (parameters) and `FcAggregateDetailPage` (domain-supplied `FcPageHeader` in every state slot, "the eight Constrained detail routes"), and § List, detail, and deep-link surfaces maps `FcAggregateDetailState` for detail routes. That covers the 2 grid routes and the 8 Constrained routes. **Agents overview, Operational status, Launch readiness, and the Audit evidence list** are FullWidth non-grid routes: no page component is named, so their loading, error, `not available`, and `Degraded` slots, and their heading/focus mechanism, are unspecified — even though the rule opening the section is "**every** route renders a non-blank localized heading with `HeadingTabIndex=-1` and a `PageTitle`, focused through `FocusHeadingAsync()`" (`442`). The Agents overview additionally has no empty or cold-load treatment of its own (it is not a grid, and "no activity" appears only as a color role in `DESIGN.md:207`). *Fix:* name the page component for those four routes and say which state-slot rule they follow, or state that they are hand-authored and carry the domain `FcPageHeader`/state pattern directly.
- **medium** **`Abandoned` reasons are incomplete and untyped.** The spine shows "reason shown (Approver, policy, Agent removed from Conversation)" (`EXPERIENCE.md:333`). FR-18 (`prd.md:391`) names four *typed* reasons carried in status and audit — `NoEligibleApprover`, `SourceConversationUnavailable`, `RemovedInConversations`, and kill switch — and requires abandoned-by-system proposals to be "excluded from the SM-3 denominator and reported separately under FR-25 and SM-C4". Two reasons are missing, none is typed, and the separate-reporting requirement reaches no Operational status row. Every architecture token needs a localized label per § Voice and Tone, so four missing tokens are four missing resource keys. *Fix:* list the four typed reasons in the `Abandoned` row and add system-abandoned to the `operational-status-panel` counts.
- **medium** **The posting-retry bound is specified in the wrong place.** The spine says "the maximum number of attempts is a `hexa` configuration field" (`EXPERIENCE.md:222`). FR-18 / A-7 (`prd.md:394`, `753`) make it a fixed bound of "at most 3 attempts over 15 minutes", owner **Architecture**, retired when Architecture confirms or retunes — not a tenant-administered field. The spine therefore invents a configuration surface (a field on `agent-config-form`, which does not list it in its inventory either) for a value the PRD does not expose. *Fix:* state the bound as `3 attempts over 15 minutes (A-7, Architecture-owned)` and render attempts-used against it read-only in the retry confirmation, rather than as a configurable field.
- **medium** **The pre-post re-validation set is reduced to its safety third.** The `retry posting` confirmation carries "the pre-post re-check statement" (`EXPERIENCE.md:231`) and § Confirmation contents names only safety. FR-18 (`prd.md:398`) requires four checks immediately before posting: the Source Conversation still exists and is accessible to the Agents Service Principal; the Agent is active and its Party identity valid; **`hexa` is still a member of and not blocked in that Conversation**; and the approved version still passes the then-current policy — "a failure moves the proposal to `PostingFailed` rather than posting stale content". Three of the four failure causes have no typed reason, no copy row, and no confirmation line. *Fix:* enumerate the four checks in the `ProposalResolution — retry posting` confirmation row and give each failure cause a `PostingFailed` typed reason in § Voice and Tone.

Both spines declare themselves "reconciled to the PRD of 2026-09-08". prd.md, which both list as a source, now carries a 2026-09-09 reconciliation whose centrepiece is FR-33, a thirteen-row authorization matrix over six named roles that OQ-21 declares authoritative for every authorization rule in the product. The consequence is not cosmetic: built strictly from these two files, a tenant administrator holding Agents.Administrator publishes the platform Content Safety Policy — and may relax it — raises their own cost caps, and grants themselves an audited override of a reached cap. prd.md:418 says of exactly these acts "so the boundary is not self-set" and "never the Tenant Agent Administrator". The 2026-09-09 Update run reconciled against the UX validation findings alone; the memlog records no PRD change signal for that date.

Separately, the two rewrites that Update was proudest of each bought their tidiness with a mechanism that does not exist. The live-region rewrite hands the entire post-submit announcement set for the product's only V1 invocation path — denied, generation failed, capacity rejected, Posted — to a pair of nodes living inside a dialog that the same sentence closes on Submit, on a seam whose two declared artifact kinds can host no persistent region anywhere else; the status badges vanish for sighted users too. The aria-disabled rule is stated spine-wide as FluentButton.DisabledFocusable and then applied to version radios, and DisabledFocusable exists on FluentButton and FluentCompoundButton only. And the editor's new dirty-state rules block Approve and the version radios "until you save or discard" while the action rail — enumerated three times — contains neither a Save nor a Discard control. Meanwhile retry posting, the one new Conversation write, was filed under operation family ProposalResolution, which in OperationGateMatrix v1 requires neither the membership/posting gate nor the safety gate. These are defects the Update introduced, and each reads as solved.

The honest summary of the last Update is therefore: it closed the mechanics it aimed at, and closed them accurately — 57 of 84 prior findings are verifiably resolved, nothing regressed, and the areas the lenses attacked hardest (provider secrets, the currency circularity, terminal-state and double-post integrity, tenant isolation, the expiry/approval race) held under re-attack.

# Governance & Audit Review — Hexalith Agents

Reviewer lens: governance, authorization, audit. Run: 2026-09-09 against the final spines updated 2026-09-08. Inputs: `DESIGN.md`, `EXPERIENCE.md`, `.memlog.md`, `reconcile-validation-2026-09-08.md`, the archived pre-update governance review, PRD (2026-09-08) and addendum, `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md`, `launch-readiness-register.md` (OperationGateMatrix v1), `external-dependency-register.md`, `epics.md` (Stories 5.3, 6.7, 7.4, 8.1 to 8.3), and the shipped `src/Hexalith.Agents.Contracts` enums and `src/Hexalith.Agents.UI` pages. Assumption throughout: a story-dev builds exactly what the spines say and nothing more; the adversary is a hostile tenant admin, a rushed approver, a second concurrent approver, a pasted cross-tenant id, a lagging projection, and an operator who would like a mistake to disappear.

## Overall verdict

The 2026-09-08 update closed the edges the previous review attacked: two approvers, reload mid-pending, foreign ids, the `0 USD` row, and the silent provider disable are now all named states with fail-closed treatment, and Success is still reserved for proven callability and a projection-confirmed post. The spines are now weakest where the *second* side effect happens: `retry posting` is a Conversation write with no surface, policy, bound, or confirmation; an `Approved` proposal is still editable and re-approvable; and the audit-governance operations that can make evidence disappear (hold release, deletion) are one role, one click, one generic sentence, and no justification. A platform-scoped Provider catalog behind a tenant-scoped `Agents.Administrator` policy is the one place a hostile tenant admin can reach across tenants, and the spine hands them a blast-radius count instead of a barrier.

## Findings

- **[high]** `retry posting` is a Conversation side effect with no surface, no `RequiredPolicy`, no operation family, no bound value, no confirmation, and no evidence rule. The lifecycle table promises a "bounded audited `retry posting` reusing the deterministic `MessageId`" and Voice offers "Retry posting or start a new Agent Call", but `proposal-editor` lists only edit, regenerate, approve, reject, abandon; `operational-status-panel` merely groups outcomes under "retry posting"; nothing says who may click it, whether it is `ProposalResolution`, whether it opens `high-impact-confirmation`, how many retries remain, or what version history and audit show per attempt. A story-dev will put a bare Retry button on the status page for `Agents.Operator`, who is not an Approver and may lack Conversation access, and FR-18's pre-post re-checks (Conversation access for the posting principal, then-current safety) will be invisible. (`EXPERIENCE.md:258`, `:184`, `:166`, `:156`, `:105`). *Fix:* add `retry posting` to the `proposal-editor` action rail for `Agents.Approver` holding current Conversation read access; family `ProposalResolution`; `high-impact-confirmation` renders the `ApprovedVersionId`, kind, author, timestamp, the typed failure reason, attempts used of the configured maximum, and the pre-post re-check statement; each attempt is a row in `version-history` (not a version) and in `audit-evidence-panel`; exhaustion removes the action and renders the sole `Start a new Agent Call`; the maximum is a `hexa` configuration field with a documented default and range, future proposals only.

- **[high]** `Approved`, `PostingPending`, and `PostingFailed` are non-terminal, and the editor's only terminal rule is "in a terminal state the editor is read-only", so edit, regenerate, and approve remain available after approval. That is a path where a version other than the approved `VersionId` can be approved and posted, or an edit lands while posting is pending; "Regenerate while any resolution is pending is `aria-disabled`" only covers the same session's lock. FR-17 requires that exactly the approved version posts and FR-18 makes `Approved` distinct from `Pending`. (`EXPERIENCE.md:156`, `:250-258`; `DESIGN.md:299`). *Fix:* once `Approved` is projection-confirmed, edit, regenerate, and approve are absent (not merely disabled); the editor pins `ApprovedVersionId` read-only; the only actions are `retry posting` (in `PostingFailed`), abandon where architecture permits, and audit; the version radio group is read-only with the approved version marked; a new decision requires a new Agent Call.

- **[high]** The governance operations that can make evidence disappear are single-role, single-click, unjustified, and confirmed by one generic sentence. `Agents.AuditOperator` inspects evidence, requests export, releases legal hold, and requests deletion on one route; `high-impact-confirmation` for `LegalHold`, `ExportRequest`, and `DeletionRequest` carries only "the action, the resource identity, the future-only effect, and the authorization basis"; FR-30 requires every governance operation to be "audited with actor and justification" and Story 8.3 distinguishes request, confirm, and cancel, yet the spine has one `request deletion` action, no justification input, no scope rendering, no hold interplay, and no rule that the deletion request is `aria-disabled` while an active hold matches. An operator who wants a mistake gone releases the hold and requests deletion in two dialogs. (`EXPERIENCE.md:62`, `:164`, `:168`; `DESIGN.md:331`; PRD FR-30; `epics.md` Story 8.3). *Fix:* a required justification field on `LegalHold` release, `ExportRequest`, `DeletionRequest`, `PolicyPublication`, and `TenantBudgetUpdate`, carried to audit; the deletion confirmation renders the exact scope (interaction ids or scope reference, content classes, item count), current hold state, the named projection list that must confirm, and an irreversibility line; deletion request `aria-disabled` with reason while a matching hold is active or `EXT-SECRETS-1` is not `Available`; a separate `confirm deletion` stage that must be performed by a different Party than the requester, or an explicit deferred item to Product + Security if a two-person rule is rejected; `audit-governance-panel` lists actor, justification, timestamps, and evidence reference for every hold, export, and deletion transition.

- **[high]** The Provider catalog is platform-scoped but gated by the tenant-scoped `Agents.Administrator` policy, so a hostile tenant admin can disable or reprice a model that every other tenant uses. The disable confirmation deliberately shows them the cross-tenant blast radius as an aggregate count "or omits it per disclosure category", which is disclosure control, not authorization. FR-19 permits cross-tenant reach only when "explicitly platform-scoped and authorized"; the spine states the scope and never states the authorization. (`EXPERIENCE.md:52`, `:154`; PRD FR-19, FR-4). *Fix:* split the route's read policy from its mutation policy: `Agents.Administrator` reads the catalog and its tenant-specific readiness and currency-mismatch view; `ProviderCatalogMutation` (create, pricing, enable, disable, secret reference) requires a platform-scoped policy constant (for example `Agents.PlatformProviderAdministrator`) registered in `AgentsFrontComposerRegistration`; the confirmation names the platform scope in the authorization-basis line; tenant admins see mutation controls absent, not disabled.

- **[high]** Configuration and governance changes have no audit surface. `audit-evidence-panel` enumerates interaction evidence only (caller, Agent, versions, safety decisions, posting outcome); FR-24 requires evidence for Agent configuration, Provider/model configuration, and every audited override; FR-32 requires cap changes to record "prior and new values"; FR-4 requires auditable Provider configuration changes. `agent-config-form` says "shows what changed where safe to expose" and policy surfaces have a History accordion item, but no component defines what a configuration-change record contains or where a `ProviderCatalogMutation`, `PolicyPublication`, `TenantBudgetUpdate`, `AgentActivation`, or override is inspectable end to end. (`EXPERIENCE.md:61`, `:152`, `:167`; `DESIGN.md:247`, `:341`; PRD FR-24, FR-32). *Fix:* add a configuration-change evidence class to `/agents/audit` and to each write surface's History item: actor, operation family, resource identity, old to new values where safe, published version, `CapabilityVersion` or policy version, projection id and version, justification, and the acceptance stages reached; overrides show scope and expiry.

- **[medium]** The currency rule is circular on a platform-scoped record. `provider-catalog-grid` says catalog currency "must equal the tenant budget currency"; `cost-control-editor` says cap currency is "equal to catalog pricing currency". Neither is the source of truth, and a platform-scoped model has one currency while tenants have many, so an editor validation of "must equal" will be evaluated against whichever tenant the operator happens to be in. (`EXPERIENCE.md:154`, `:162`). *Fix:* the tenant budget currency set on Cost controls is authoritative and must be configured before caps; the catalog editor validates only ISO 4217 well-formedness; a per-tenant mismatch renders `status-important` on that tenant's readiness view and blocks reservations as a readiness blocker; changing the budget currency after settled spend exists is a `TenantBudgetUpdate` whose confirmation states that settled spend is not converted.

- **[medium]** Rate-limit refusal has no Agent-call state. FR-32 requires the UI to expose "the reason when a call or regeneration is refused for exceeding" a per-Party or per-Conversation rate limit, but the Agent call table maps only `budget blocked` (caps, missing pricing) and `capacity rejected` (concurrency), and the must-grow list for `AgentCallOperationStatus` has no rate-limit value. A story-dev will fold it into `budget blocked` and point users at Cost controls to raise a cap that is not the problem. (`EXPERIENCE.md:178`, `:237-239`, `:166`). *Fix:* add `rate limited` (`RateLimited`) with the window and scope (Party or Conversation) in the safe reason, `status-severe`, recovery "wait for the window", counted on Operational status; add the value to the must-grow list.

- **[medium]** The automatic path borrows proposal tokens for posting. UJ-2 says "the call enters `PostingPending`" and "renders the projection whose outcome is `Posted`", but those are `ProposedAgentReplyState` values; `AgentCallOperationStatus` ends at `Generated`, the shipped `AgentInteractionStatusView` exposes no posting outcome, and the must-grow list omits `PostingPending`, `Posted`, `PostingFailed` for the automatic path. Story 6.7 requires "posting pending, posted, and posting failed" as distinct automatic statuses. (`EXPERIENCE.md:177-178`, `:242-244`, `:439-444`; `src/.../AgentCallOperationStatus.cs`, `AgentInteractionStatusView.cs`). *Fix:* add `PostingPending`, `Posted`, `PostingFailed` (with typed reason and the same bounded retry rule, actor `Agents.Operator` or the caller) to the `AgentCallOperationStatus` must-grow list and to the Agent call table; name the `agent-interaction-status` field that carries the posted `MessageId`.

- **[medium]** The nearing-expiry threshold is not in the spines. Both spines render a `status-warning` chip for "nearing expiry" and the badge takes a "nearing-expiry flag", but the 10 % of window or 1 hour rule lives only in the reconciliation and memlog. A story-dev reading the spines alone will invent a fixed number that is wrong for a 1-hour or a 30-day window. (`EXPERIENCE.md:150`, `:155`; `DESIGN.md:197`, `:275`; `reconcile-validation-2026-09-08.md § 3`). *Fix:* state in the `proposal-state-badge` row: nearing expiry begins at 10 % of the proposal's configured window or 1 hour before `ExpiresAt`, whichever is smaller; the flag is server-computed against `ExpiresAt`.

- **[medium]** Restricted safety categories are permitted "only with ... Confirmation Response Mode", but switching the mode to Automatic is a separate `AgentSetupMutation` whose confirmation says nothing about them. After the switch, restricted content either posts automatically (wrong side effect) or every call fails safety with no visible cause. (`EXPERIENCE.md:151`, `:161`; PRD FR-26). *Fix:* `response-mode-toggle` confirmation states that any permitted restricted category is blocked while Automatic Response Mode is active, or activation and mode change are blocked with the blocker "restricted categories permitted; Confirmation Response Mode required"; `content-safety-policy-editor` shows the effective mode beside each restricted row.

- **[medium]** Per-Conversation `safety blocked` history on `/agents/status` is rendered to `Agents.Operator` with no Conversation-access rule, while `audit-evidence-panel` requires current Conversation read access for Conversation-derived evidence. Conversation identity plus "permanently blocked for unsafe historical content" is itself Conversation-derived information about a specific Conversation, visible to an operator who may not be a participant. (`EXPERIENCE.md:166` versus `:167`; PRD OQ-18, FR-24). *Fix:* the history row shows an opaque Conversation reference and blocked-call count; the Conversation name or link renders only when the operator holds current read access; otherwise the row is `existence only`.

- **[medium]** The harness removal condition is weaker than the story that must prove it. The spine says Story 6.7 "removes or blocks" `/agents/conversation-call`; Story 6.7 requires alternate invocation entries to be "absent" and names `AlternateInvocationGuardTests`. A blocked route is still an alternate entry with an `[Authorize]` surface. Meanwhile the shipped registration still lists the route at nav order 4 and the shipped terminal-proposal `Start a new Agent Call` link targets the harness; the spine never says where `Start a new Agent Call` goes once the harness is gone, nor what policy guards the harness while it exists. (`EXPERIENCE.md:68`, `:156`, `:261`; `src/Hexalith.Agents.UI/Composition/AgentsFrontComposerRegistration.cs:139-147`; `src/Hexalith.Agents.UI/Components/Pages/ProposalDetail.razor:227`). *Fix:* "removed: route unregistered and page deleted before Story 6.7 closes; `AlternateInvocationGuardTests` proves no Agents-owned route accepts an Agent Call"; while it exists the harness carries `Agents.Administrator`, is excluded from `LR-UI-CONFORMANCE` scope, and writes nothing in production-like profiles; `Start a new Agent Call` navigates to the Source Conversation where **Call hexa** lives.

- **[medium]** `high-impact-confirmation` defines required contents for only three families. Proposal approval, pricing, and Provider disable get explicit contents; `PolicyPublication`, `TenantBudgetUpdate`, `AgentActivation`, `AgentSetupMutation`, `LegalHold`, and `ExportRequest` get the generic sentence. FR-32 requires prior and new cap values; a policy publication must show the version about to become current and the categories that change; an export must show tenant scope, time range, content classes, and the artifact expiry; activation must show the evaluated gate set and `RegistryRevision`. (`EXPERIENCE.md:168`, `:161-164`, `:152`). *Fix:* add a per-family required-contents table to the `high-impact-confirmation` row; the rendered contents are the same values the audit record will carry.

- **[medium]** Approver-policy publication is "future-only" while AD-12 resolves approver rights from the interaction's policy snapshot, so a revoked approver keeps authority over every pending proposal and the admin who just published the revocation is told the opposite. (`EXPERIENCE.md:153`, `:330`; `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md` AD-12). *Fix:* the `PolicyPublication` confirmation states "pending proposals keep the Approver Policy they were created under; abandon them to revoke"; the Approver policy surface shows the count of pending proposals under each prior policy version with a link to the queue filtered by policy version.

- **[medium]** Export and deletion controls have no fail-closed rendering while `EXT-SECRETS-1` is `Uncommitted`, and the export panel never states artifact expiry or that no plaintext is ever rendered. Stories 8.2 and 8.3 are blocked on the seam; the panel as written lets a story-dev render enabled Export and Delete buttons that submit into a dependency that cannot resolve keys. (`EXPERIENCE.md:164`; `DESIGN.md:331`; `external-dependency-register.md § EXT-SECRETS-1`; `epics.md` Stories 8.2, 8.3). *Fix:* export and deletion actions render `aria-disabled` with `authority unresolved` treatment naming the dependency while `EXT-SECRETS-1` is not `Available`; export rows show manifest state, exclusive expiry, and download availability; the panel never renders artifact contents or keys.

- **[low]** The override is "bounded" with no bound. `cost-control-editor` records actor, justification, scope, and expiry, but not the amount or maximum, whether override spend is reserved and reconciled like any other, or where the active override is visible to the Operator. (`EXPERIENCE.md:162`, `:489`). *Fix:* the override carries a numeric ceiling and an expiry both shown in the confirmation; Operational status shows the active override with remaining amount and time.

- **[low]** Pricing version authorship is unspecified. FR-4 makes a decreased or reused pricing version a typed rejection; the spine pins the column and shows the effective version in the confirmation but never says whether the operator types it or the server assigns it, nor how the typed rejection renders. (`EXPERIENCE.md:154`, `:168`). *Fix:* server-assigned, displayed as "next pricing version {n}" in the confirmation; a rejection renders `superseded by another decision` with the current version.

- **[low]** `Expired` "rendered whenever `ExpiresAt` has passed on any read" does not name the clock. A client-clock comparison shows `Expired` early or late and lets Approve stay enabled until `expired at approval`. (`EXPERIENCE.md:261`, `:270`). *Fix:* the comparison uses the server time carried on the read (`CreatedAt` is already server time per `proposal-queue-grid`); the client never uses its own wall clock for governance state.

- **[low]** "Operator-only visible" for `ConfigurationReferenceId` is a disclosure category, not a role, on a page whose only policy is `Agents.Administrator`. (`EXPERIENCE.md:154`, `:352`). *Fix:* name the policy that may see the masked reference and its configured state, and confirm it is a subset of whoever may mutate.

- **[low]** `needs my action` has no state definition; a proposal another Approver has already moved to `Approved` or `PostingFailed` may still be counted and listed under it. (`EXPERIENCE.md:155`, `:165`). *Fix:* `needs my action` is `Pending`, `Edited`, `Regenerated` where the viewer is an authorized Approver who did not last edit the selected version, plus `PostingFailed` with retries remaining.

- **[low]** Reload re-derives the pending lock "from the authoritative status projection", but for `ProposalResolution` no projection carries a pending-command notion; if `proposal-detail` has not caught up the lock is not re-armed and the row looks idle. EventStore concurrency still protects the outcome, so the impact is a confusing `superseded by another decision` rather than a wrong side effect. (`EXPERIENCE.md:290`, `:188`). *Fix:* name the projection and field per family, or render `catching up` with resolution controls `aria-disabled` whenever the read's projection version is below the last accepted write's expected version.

## What is solid

- Success is reserved for proven truth on every surface: `Posted`, proven `callable` at a `RegistryRevision`, current `Pass`, `Ready / Callable / None` within `ValidUntil`, or `ProjectionConfirmed`; `Approved` and `PostingPending` are progress; `AuthoritativePending` is durable and never re-submitted; catch-up exhaustion is `awaiting projection`, never Success and never `Stale`.
- The proposal lifecycle matches the shipped `ProposedAgentReplyState` name for name, terminal states are read-only with resolution actions absent, `Expired` is applied on read regardless of timer delivery, and every display-only outcome (`superseded by another decision`, `expired at approval`, `safety blocked at approval`, `existence only`, `stale proposal`, `authority unresolved`) fails closed with controls `aria-disabled`.
- Segregation of duties is enforced at the point of approval and at policy configuration: dirty editor, last-editor, and sole-Approver-of-own-call all block; the confirmation renders the exact `VersionId`, kind, author, timestamp, and content that will post; the safety re-check on the exact selected version is stated.
- Pricing governance is now explicit: no defaults, typed zero with a warning that it reserves nothing, pinned pricing version, `CapabilityVersion` as the concurrency token, old to new and effective version in the confirmation, future-only copy.
- Provider secrets cannot enter the UI: reference only, masked, autocomplete off, never echoed, never in URLs, clipboard, diagnostics, accessible names, or announcements.
- Tenant isolation is one indistinguishable `not available` state for unauthorized, foreign-tenant, and non-existent ids, with `[Authorize]` on every page and tenant-scoped filter suggestions; the pending count only counts readable proposals; the Conversation seam fails closed on membership, denies before Provider work, and treats Conversation content as untrusted data.
- Cost control has no implicit defaults, an unconfigured cap or rate limit is a readiness blocker, thresholds evaluate settled plus reserved spend with both figures labelled, and `Unknown` outcomes hold their reservation and are never retried.
- Launch readiness never fills a missing record, never falls back to an older `Pass`, never issues the `RQ-1` decision, and production enablement is a separate indicator from qualification callability.

## Overall verdict

The predecessor's list is largely **closed** — 17 of its 25 findings are resolved outright, including every one it filed against the register and `epics.md`. `EXT-CONV-UI-1` now carries three artifact kinds and `ConsumingStories: 6.7; RQ-1`; `epics.md` Story 6.7 now declares the seam and owns the harness deletion by AC; the four-component FC-TBL mandate is relabelled Agents-owned with the envelope rules named; `FcFilterResetButton` and `FcStatusFilterChips` are correctly dropped with the Fluxor reasoning stated; `FcExpandInRowDetail`'s four `[EditorRequired]` parameters are exact to the file; § Shipped-code corrections grew from eleven rows to sixteen and **every one of the sixteen is true against the code today**, including the reverted `Agents.PlatformOperator` row, the two missing `NotAvailable` keys, the zero-CSS row, and zero `DisabledFocusable` in `src/`. `sprint-status.yaml` has been regenerated across all four epics. The AD-30 revert landed cleanly: `Agents.ComplianceInspector` appears in no artifact and no source file, and § Authorization roles' five-constant / six-role mapping matches AD-30 and the shipped registration character for character.

What the Update introduced instead is a **story-ownership fault, and it is the worst class of defect this lens exists to catch.** `epics.md` carries 33 stories; the pair's ownership tables name 28. The five it never names — **5.8, 5.9, 5.10, 6.8, 7.7** — are live backlog rows in `sprint-status.yaml`, and three of them own precisely the things the Update added and left unowned or misassigned. Story 6.8 owns the additive runtime reasons the spine hands to "6.3, 6.4, 6.5"; Story 7.7 owns the entire `PostingFailed` recovery lane the Update's new rail rows describe, including a mandatory pre-retry existence read, `Posted(LateConfirmed)`, and a kill-switch **pause of the very retry window the spine tells the confirmation to count down**; Story 5.10 owns the `Caller` retirement the spine assigns to 5.4 alone. Worse, two of the Update's headline additions have no owning story at all with an acceptance criterion that would build them: the **per-tenant kill switch** (Story 8.7 is inspection-only and never mentions it; Story 8.4 owns the aggregate field with no command, confirmation, or trigger AC) and the **Security approval stage on `PolicyPublication`**, which additionally *contradicts* the Architecture Spine, where Security approval is "a recorded Security approval reference in the `PolicyPublication` command" — the publisher-supplied field the spine explicitly forbids. And the Update declared two new `high-impact-confirmation` families without naming them or giving them a § Confirmation contents row, while both registers already carry family tokens for them.

Nothing here is a vocabulary error. A dev can build the badges, the grids, and the dialogs. What a dev cannot do without inventing decisions is build the kill switch, the Security approval stage, the retry lane, or the per-Conversation block — four of the Update's own additions.

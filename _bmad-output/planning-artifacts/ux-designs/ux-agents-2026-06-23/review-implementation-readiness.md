# Implementation Readiness Review — Hexalith Agents — 2026-09-12 (round 5)

Lens: the story-dev — human or AI — who must build any story in Epics 5–8 from `DESIGN.md` (`final`, `updated: 2026-09-10`) + `EXPERIENCE.md` (`final`, `updated: 2026-09-12`) plus the named sources, starting from `src/Hexalith.Agents.UI`, with **no designer to ask and no mockup or wireframe to consult**. One question: can they build it without asking?

Verified this run against: the whole of `src/Hexalith.Agents.UI` (11 pages, 20 shared components, 42 gateway types, `Resources/AgentsResources.resx`/`.fr.resx` at 599 keys each); `Composition/AgentsFrontComposerRegistration.cs`; `epics.md` at its 33-story active backlog (Stories 5.1–5.10, 6.1–6.8, 7.1–7.7, 8.1–8.8); `launch-readiness-register.md` (`OperationGateMatrixVersion = 3`); the FrontComposer reference tree and `docs/reference/components/{front-composer-shell,navigation,datagrid}.md`; `FrontComposerNavEntry`, `FrontComposerNavigation`, `FcFluentIcons`, `FcAggregateListPage`, `FcAggregateDetailPage`, `FcAggregateDetailState`, `FcExpandInRowDetail`, `FcFilterEmptyState`, `FcStatusFilterChips`, `FcFilterResetButton`, `FcPageHeader`, `FcDestructiveConfirmationDialog`, `IBadgeCountService`, `ReflectionActionQueueProjectionCatalog`, `IProjectionChangeDetailNotifier`, `FrontComposerShell.razor`; the Tenants precedent (`Hexalith.Tenants.UI`); `reconcile-validation-2026-09-10.md`; the prior round's `.working/validation-2026-09-10-round4/review-implementation-readiness.md`; and the newly-landed `architecture-agents-2026-06-23-2/reviews/reconcile-ux-2026-09-12.md` and `reviews/review-2026-09-12-verified-current.md`.

## Overall verdict

**Adequate.** This is a markedly stronger pair than round 4 reviewed: the round-4 critical about five unknown stories is largely closed, the round-4 high findings about the `PostingFailed` lane, `PausedDuration`, `LateConfirmed`, the `MessageId` pre-exit lookup, the `TenantKillSwitch` and block/clear confirmation families, the `agents.launch-readiness` `ViewKey`, the Security-approval two-stage invention, and the `EXT-CONV-UI-1` seam row are all closed and closed correctly. The four contradictions the 2026-09-12 architecture reconciliation asked UX to absorb — the six-conjunct Eligible Approver predicate, the forbidden ordinal queue position, the retry actor, and the stale `OperationGateMatrixVersion = 2` citations — are three-and-a-half absorbed. On state machines, authorization, concurrency, confirmation contents and accessibility the spine is not merely buildable, it is more precise than most codebases. A dev building the proposal lane, the Agent-call lane, the Conversation Agent State machine, or any `high-impact-confirmation` will not need to ask a question.

The weakness is concentrated exactly where the no-mockup decision bites hardest, and the decision is what makes it matter. **Composition of the read-and-inspect surfaces is under-determined, and four of the fifteen IA surfaces have no view-model contract at all.** A dev handed Story 5.2 must invent the entire Agents overview body; a dev handed Story 8.8 must invent an audit-evidence grid the spine claims to govern and gives no `ViewKey`, no columns, no sort and no page size; a dev handed Story 5.4 must invent the Party picker that makes the Approver Policy builder usable. Two named components — `proposal-notification` and, by omission, `AgentsPageStatusRegion` — cannot be built as written: the first is placed inside a shell nav link the spine itself proves is un-parameterizable, the second is the sole live-region carrier on every route and exists outside the 22-component three-place contract with no visual spec, no behavior row and no props. And the route-gating rule that C-1 introduced to fix a real authorization defect — route-level gating on the *disjunction* of two tiers — has no mechanism behind it: `FrontComposerNavEntry.RequiredPolicy` is one nullable string, consumed by one `<AuthorizeView Policy="…">`, and `[Authorize(Policy = …)]` takes one name. That is a rule a dev must guess at, on the FR-19 cross-tenant surface, where a wrong guess ships.

Copy is the other structural gap, and it is an honest one rather than an oversight: § Voice and Tone's ~42 EN/FR pairs are excellent and none of them is a page title, heading, nav label, back-link label, field label, filter label or enum label. The "per-enum inventory obligation rather than a hand-maintained list" decision is right in principle and leaves a dev writing several hundred bilingual strings with no key list to work from, against a shipped `.resx` of 599 keys the spine neither blesses nor inventories.

Verdict per area is below. Nothing here reverses a round-4 fix; the criticals are new surface area exposed by the round-4 fixes, not regressions of them.

## Findings

### Critical

- **[critical]** **Route-level gating on a disjunction of policy tiers has no buildable mechanism, on the surfaces where getting it wrong is an authorization defect.** § Information Architecture: "**A surface carrying operations of two authorization tiers is gated at the route on the _disjunction_ of the tiers, and each control on its own constant** … The route-level `[Authorize(Policy = ...)]` admits the union." Verified in the shell: `FrontComposerNavEntry` (`references/Hexalith.FrontComposer/src/Hexalith.FrontComposer.Contracts/Registration/FrontComposerNavEntry.cs`) declares `string? RequiredPolicy` — one nullable string — and `FrontComposerNavigation.razor:43-45` consumes it as a single `<AuthorizeView Policy="@entry.RequiredPolicy">`. ASP.NET's `[Authorize(Policy = …)]` likewise names one policy. AD-30 names five constants, none of them a union, and § Authorization roles says AD-30 is the naming authority and that renaming an identifier "requires an AD-30 amendment, not a UX decision" — so the dev may not mint `Agents.StatusRead`. The four affected routes are Operational status, Launch readiness, Audit evidence and (split read/write) Provider catalog, Content safety policy and Cost controls. The three paths open to a dev are: invent a composite constant (forbidden), register duplicate nav entries per constant (produces duplicate rail tiles, and the spine's nav-order table has one row per surface), or gate on the narrowest constant — which is exactly the defect C-1 was written to close, and it is what the tree does today. *Fix:* state the mechanism. Either name the composite policy constants and file them as an AD-30 amendment with an owning story, or state that the route is gated on the **broadest** constant with every control on its own, and file the nav-entry duplication question as a FrontComposer request alongside the six outstanding glyph requests.

- **[critical]** **`proposal-notification` is specified into a location that cannot host it, and the spine proves this in its own row.** `DESIGN.md § proposal-notification`: "A domain-rendered `FluentBadge` in `{colors.status-informative}` **inside the Agents overview link**". `EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns`, same component: "The count is domain-rendered **inside the Agents overview link** as the whole string `{count} proposals pending approval`" — and, four sentences later, "`FrontComposerNavigation` exposes **no `[Parameter]` at all**, and the nav badge renders as a bare `FluentBadge` with no label slot." Both halves verified: `FrontComposerNavigation.razor(.cs)` declares no `[Parameter]` whatsoever (only `[Inject]` members), and nav badges render as `<FluentBadge Appearance=Filled Color=Brand>@count</FluentBadge>` with no label slot, gated on `count > 0` over `ReflectionActionQueueProjectionCatalog`'s `ProjectionRole.ActionQueue` enumeration. There is no seam by which the Agents domain renders anything inside a shell nav entry. So the one in-product discovery affordance FR-13 and Story 7.1 require has no location: not the rail (un-extensible), and the row explicitly rules out the shell count. A 5.2 or 7.1 dev must invent one. *Fix:* relocate the count to a surface Agents owns — the Agents overview page body's readiness summary is the obvious candidate and the Story 7.1 AC already says "in-product pending count" without naming the rail — or file the localizable-label slot as the FrontComposer request the row already contemplates and state the interim location explicitly.

- **[critical]** **The Agents overview — the default Agents surface — has no field inventory, and its second accordion item exists only as two words in a table cell.** `DESIGN.md § Layout & Spacing` gives it "Readiness summary" primary / "Blockers; Recent activity" accordion. § Information Architecture gives it a route, a policy, a layout, three stories and three contracts. Nowhere in either spine is there a field list, an order, a grouping, a row shape, a count, a paging rule, an empty state or a source contract for **Recent activity**; the string "Recent activity" occurs exactly once in the pair. The nearest thing to a spec is UJ-1 step 12 — "Provider/model, lifecycle, mode, callability, `EnvironmentProfile`, freshness, and safe blockers" — which is a journey resolution, not a field inventory, and covers the readiness summary only. `AgentsOverview.razor` today ships a `<dl>` of seven facts and no accordion at all, so the dev has neither a spine table nor a conforming precedent. Two competent devs will ship visibly different landing pages, and this is the route UX-DR2 makes the domain's default. *Fix:* add an Agents overview row to a per-surface field table, or delete "Recent activity" and say the overview carries readiness and blockers only.

- **[critical]** **The Audit evidence list is a fourth grid that § Grid rules claims to govern and cannot: no `ViewKey`, no columns, no sort, no page size, no 320 px set.** § Information Architecture, Audit evidence: "It **additionally carries an enumerable, tenant-scoped list** for the two classes that hold no Conversation content … The Agents grid rules apply to that list and to it only." § Grid rules' `ViewKey` row enumerates exactly three: `agents.provider-catalog`, `agents.pending-proposals`, `agents.launch-readiness`, and adds "Each mandatory component takes one, **view-key mismatches fail closed**." `FcExpandInRowDetail` marks `ViewKey`, `HasExpanded`, `ChildContent` and `DetailPanelAriaLabel` all `[Parameter][EditorRequired]`, and `FcFilterEmptyState` does the same for `ViewKey`/`TotalCount`/`EntityPlural` (both verified at `references/Hexalith.FrontComposer/src/Hexalith.FrontComposer.Shell/Components/DataGrid/`). A Story 8.8 dev has no key to pass, no localized detail-panel label in the inventory, no column set, no default sort, no page size (the queue's 25 is stated; this list's is not), and no surviving-column set at 320 px — while the surface must render the `UnreviewedInspection` flag and "the inspection rate", which § Audit evidence rules makes the reason the list exists at all. This is the identical defect round 4 closed for the Launch readiness gate grid, reopened one surface over. *Fix:* add `agents.audit-evidence` to the `ViewKey` row with its `DetailPanelAriaLabel`, and give the list a columns/sort/page-size/320 px paragraph in § Audit evidence rules the way § Proposal queue rules and § Provider catalog rules each carry one.

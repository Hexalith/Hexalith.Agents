# Product behavior review · November 20, 2026

**Verdict.** Three requirements need clarification before their acceptance criteria can yield consistent results. The current source also shows two gaps in posting behavior. The November module checks and the external release decision have separate scopes.

The review compared the working PRD with source and test definitions at Agents `05ddee8`. It covered call admission, identity and tenant scope, context measurement, posting and approval, evidence disclosure, readiness, retention and product metrics. Line references identify the reviewed revision. This was a static review.

## Findings in the PRD

**B1 · High · Early denials cannot produce the required context measurements.** FR-8 checks caller authority first, can reject on a local Conversation block, and reaches context measurement only at step 7 (PRD lines 293–301). FR-9 and FR-24 require measured size, budget, capability version and context mode “for every call,” including blocked calls (lines 317, 323–325 and 646). A request denied before context access has no permissible measurement. The aggregate already records the early gate decision and blockers separately (`src/Hexalith.Agents/AgentInteraction/AgentInteractionState.cs` lines 152–164). Requiring a size for this case would reward an unauthorized read or a fabricated measurement in an audit. Limit the measurement requirement to calls that reached that step. For earlier stops, require the safe gate and rejection reason and mark the measurement as unevaluated or absent. Test an early denial and an authorized oversize read separately.

**B2 · High · A failed post can stay unresolved beyond the retention example.** FR-18 says a `PostingFailed` proposal “always reaches the §9 retention clock” (line 476). An unavailable or conflicting message lookup refuses its exit and preserves `PostingFailed` even after the posting window elapses (lines 470 and 478). For automatic calls the linked interaction stays nonterminal, so its 365-day clock has not started (lines 351–355 and 968). For confirmation calls, `ProposalCreated` is already a terminal *call* status while the proposal can still be pending (lines 307, 440 and 968). The implementation records those two facts together (`AgentInteractionState.cs` lines 288–300). Two retention implementations could therefore choose different start instants, or discard content from a still-open proposal. State the event that starts retention for confirmation mode, distinguish it from call creation, and qualify eventual closure on an authoritative lookup. Keep the safe nonterminal state during prolonged unavailability. Include an interrupted post with a prolonged lookup outage in the retention fixture.

**B3 · Medium · SM-7 excludes an unedited rejection under a literal reading.** SM-3 identifies rejection as a human decision, and FR-18 makes the rejection transition terminal (PRD lines 440, 453 and 1048). SM-7 counts proposals “edited, regenerated, or rejected before that decision” (line 1049). A first-action rejection occurs at the decision. The aggregate records that one event as the rejection (`AgentInteractionState.cs` lines 502–511). Two calculators can disagree on whether the rejection counts, changing the launch-health result for the 10–60% band. Define the numerator as distinct decided proposals with a preceding edit or regeneration, or whose decision was rejection. Include direct rejection and edit-then-rejection cases to verify each contributes once.

## Implementation findings

**B4 · High · The source adapter can confirm a message without its provenance.** FR-11 requires the participant-visible AI marker and call reference, FR-17 requires human-edit and editor attribution, and §8 assigns their transport to Conversations (PRD lines 356–359, 433–436 and 891). `src/Hexalith.Agents.Server/Ports/ConversationClientResponsePoster.cs` lines 165–173 explicitly accept null provenance and otherwise check only the AI flag and trace reference. The synthetic recovery fixture returns null provenance and expects `Posted` (`test/Hexalith.Agents.Server.Tests/ConversationClientResponsePosterTests.cs` lines 288–315 and 357–360). This demonstrates recovery and deduplication but cannot establish complete posted attribution. In Stories 6.6 and 7.4, carry the selected version's required provenance through the owner contract and require a matching durable observation before reporting success. Include missing provenance and incorrect editor identity in the owner fixture.

**B5 · High · Approval can be sent for recording after the append.** FR-17 and FR-18 specify approval of the selected version, then posting, then final confirmation (PRD lines 430–436 and 452–459). Story 7.4 makes the required ordering explicit. Only the committed pending event authorizes the append (`_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/epics.md` lines 2617–2623). The source orchestrator invokes the append before dispatching the approval result (`src/Hexalith.Agents.Server/Application/AgentInteractions/AgentInteractionProposalApprovalOrchestrator.cs` lines 95–105 and 164–176). The existing aggregate test expects approval, posting pending and posting success from one result (`test/Hexalith.Agents.Tests/AgentInteractionProposalApprovalAggregateTests.cs` lines 22–38). An interruption between the external append and the Agents write can leave a Conversation message without its durable approval trail. Keep the existing PRD guarantee. Make the Story 7.4 fixture demonstrate the separately committed approval and posting authorization, then owner confirmation. Include a restart after each boundary.

## Discovery Research Notes

- Slack AI focuses on summarizing channels, threads, and DMs, and on generating recaps, with workspace-level admin controls for AI features. Hexalith Agents should differentiate through an explicit, named AI participant identity, tenant-safe Conversation attribution, and confirmation workflows rather than generic summarization. Source: https://slack.com/help/articles/25076892548883-Guide-to-AI-features-in-Slack and https://slack.com/help/articles/28244420881555-Manage-access-to-AI-features-in-Slack
- Microsoft 365 Copilot in Teams supports meeting summaries, action items, and live or post-meeting questions. Hexalith Agents is closer to governed participation in asynchronous Conversations than to meeting assistance, which is why the PRD defines Source Conversation context, caller identity, and posting authority precisely — as the Conversation Context Policy, the Agent Call record, and the Approver Policy resolved through the Conversation Facilitator. Source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/teams/copilot/catch-up-on-meetings-with-microsoft-365-copilot-in-teams
- Zoom AI Companion includes chat summarization and message composition using Conversation context. Hexalith Agents' Confirmation Response Mode needs a sharper approval boundary: Proposed Agent Replies are not Conversation Messages until approved. Source: https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0057623
- Atlassian Rovo Agents are configurable AI teammates with knowledge sources and plugins. They can be used in chat, automation rules, and editing workflows. Hexalith Agents V1 deliberately defers tools, long-term memory, ambient triggers, and project/folder activation to prove governed participation first. Source: https://support.atlassian.com/rovo/docs/agents/

## Options Considered in the 2026-09-09 Reconciliation

Rejected alternatives from the validation reconciliation, kept here so downstream readers know what was weighed. `prd.md` governs, and the OQ, SM, SM-C, A, FR, EXT, and RQ identifiers below resolve to its registers.

- **SM-3 and the `RQ-1` gate.** Three repairs competed:
  - keep SM-3 as a gate metric re-expressed as human-decision latency;
  - keep it as a gate metric defined relative to each Agent's expiry ("decision within min(expiry, 26 hours)");
  - cap the OQ-3 expiry range at 24 hours for launch tenants.

  All three left a metric in the gate that only real usage can produce. The PRD instead splits pre-enablement gate metrics from launch-health metrics (OQ-22), keeps SM-3 as a launch-health primary defined as human-decision latency, and moves the expiry rate to counter-metric SM-C5. The 80%/24-hour threshold is provisional (A-13).
- **Status label.** Relabeling the PRD as `approved-pending` or `draft` was rejected because the defects that justified it (OQ-19, an undated OQ-18) were resolved in the same run; §0 now separates requirements authority from build readiness instead.
- **Safe Context Budget.** Aligning the PRD down to the shipped formula (input limit minus output allowance) was rejected because it reintroduces post-reservation Provider overflow; the PRD formula was extended to include the prompt and framing, and the shipped formula declared non-conformant.
- **Blocked context mode.** Recording blocked calls as context mode `Unknown` plus a reason was rejected in favor of an additive `Blocked` value, so the FR-23 sentinel stays meaningless everywhere.
- **Facilitator wire name.** Adding `ConversationFacilitator` and deprecating `ConversationOwner` was rejected as churn with no behavioral gain; the legacy identifier is recorded and the no-owner rule scoped to human-readable text.
- **Membership seam.** A transitional "verified at posting until EXT-CONV-AI-1 lands" clause was rejected; the PRD states the target sequence and names posting-time-only verification non-conformant, leaving the interim to the dependency register.
- **Removal notification.** Requiring Hexalith.Conversations to notify Agents of participant removal was rejected in favor of an Agents-owned per-Conversation block, which needs no new Conversations seam.
- **Disclosure category.** Aligning the PRD to the code's one-required-category-per-policy shape was rejected (a medium-severity drift finding, left unapplied); the PRD keeps the per-source rule with an operator-only default as a requirement.

## Options Considered — Second 2026-09-09 Update

Rejected alternatives (and one deferral) from the decisions recorded as OQ-24 through OQ-30, raised while applying the validation report and at the reviewer gate on the updated PRD. As above, `prd.md` governs and its identifiers resolve to its registers; in addition, the Spine is the Architecture Spine cited in §8.1, AD identifiers are its decisions, and M identifiers are medium-severity findings from the adversarial reviewer.

- **Freezing `ExpiresAt` while the kill switch is pulled.** The adversarial reviewer proposed freezing expiry and the retry window together. Rejected in favor of the Spine AD-12 rule (retry clock paused, expiry keeps running): a proposal that waits out a suspension is stale by construction, and letting it expire avoids the weeks-old-content problem the same review raised elsewhere (its deferred M-12).
- **A `Suspended` lifecycle state.** Rejected in favor of a `Suspended` status reported by FR-25 while the lifecycle state stays `Draft`/`Active`/`Disabled`: the kill switch is a tenant fact, not an Agent fact, and a lifecycle value would have to be cleared on release and could collide with a genuine `Disabled`.
- **Amending the Spine so the Tenant Agent Administrator creates `hexa`.** Rejected; FR-1 and FR-33 were aligned to the Spine's Platform-principal provisioning instead, because the Spine rule was approved after the FR-1 text and a create-only, idempotent Platform provision is the simpler guarantee of exactly one `hexa` per tenant.
- **Registering the SM-2 event feed as a seventh Conversations seam.** Rejected; the feed is stated as an alternative form of seam 4 so that the A-4 fallback is inside a registered seam without adding a register entry from the PRD side.
- **Deleting the A-4 fallback outright.** Rejected because Spine AD-28 already relies on it.
- **A per-tenant Provider secret reference as an alternative to data-handling terms.** Rejected for V1 (§6.2): the platform-owned account plus a data-handling record and tenant acceptance closes the finding without a new credential path.
- **Scanning human edits at edit time (adversarial M-2) and the other medium-severity findings not co-located with an applied fix.** Deferred, not rejected; see this update's report for the deferred list.
- **Making the hourly re-check cadence non-configurable.** Rejected; a bounded range (A-20) lets qualification environments run it faster without a contract change.
- **Dropping the `Approved → Abandoned` row to match Spine AD-5.** The reviewer gate offered two ways to close the abandon-in-flight hole: make `PostingPending` uninterruptible and keep system abandonment from `Approved` on a detected removal, or delete that row and let the pre-post re-validation route the proposal through `PostingFailed`. The first was chosen: a removed `hexa` can never post, so leaving an approved proposal to fail and then be abandoned by hand adds a step without adding safety. AD-5 was corrected in the Spine's second 2026-09-09 update to state the same transition; the human abandon from `Approved` and the `PostingWindowElapsed` bound added by the third update are a new PRD-originated amendment pending in the Spine (§8.1).
- **Letting `Approved` proposals post through a suspension.** FR-28 and Spine AD-12 said `Approved` proposals "complete or fail on their own terms" under the kill switch; the FR-18 pre-post guard contradicted that. Safety won: an `Approved` proposal waits, consuming no retry attempt, because the SM-4 trigger is a confirmed security event and posting approved content through it defeats the switch. AD-12 was corrected in the Spine's second 2026-09-09 update to state the same rule.
- **A host-asserted availability signal for FR-34.** Rejected after the reviewer gate showed a wrapper around the no-op default could report itself as production; replaced by a seal-unseal-erase attestation checked against the register's committed engine identity and version.
- **Platform-recorded tenant acceptance of Provider data-handling terms.** Rejected because no tenant Party would have acted; acceptance is now a Tenant Agent Administrator action against a named `DataHandlingVersion`, with Platform enablement reduced to permitting selection.
- **Evaluating the kill-switch trigger over the platform cohort for small tenants.** Rejected in favor of a scaled minimum sample (for tenants with fewer than 5 Parties, every calling Party and 20 calls), which keeps the trigger per tenant.

## Options Considered — Third 2026-09-09 Update

Rejected alternatives from the decisions taken while applying the 2026-09-09T11:01Z validation report. Those decisions' PRD-side outcomes are FR amendments, the new OQ-31, and assumptions A-22 through A-26. As above, `prd.md` governs and its identifiers resolve to its registers; the Spine is the Architecture Spine cited in §8.1, and AD identifiers are its decisions.

- **Acknowledgement-only handling of a late `ARCH-A` row.** Under the prior rule, a row added after the scheduling of the `RQ-1` evaluation blocked only once the Release PM acknowledged it, so the gate failed open on silence. Rejected; the default is inverted, and a late row blocks unless the Release PM records a `DeferredAssumption` deferral naming the row, its owner, the FR consequences not exercised, and a revisit date (FR-28, §8.1).
- **Renaming "Release PM" to Release Operator.** Folding every use of "Release PM" into the Release Operator role was rejected: the launch decision's accountable owner and the operator of the kill switch are different accountabilities, so the Release PM keeps its name, gains a glossary entry, and holds two FR-33 rows recorded in the launch readiness register rather than on the public surface.
- **Clear-only re-admission after an external removal.** Letting only the FR-33 clear row re-admit `hexa` from `ExternallyRemoved` was rejected in favor of presence-in-Conversations re-admission, gated by A-22 (Conversations restricts adding an AI participant to Facilitators or the administrative role); until A-22 is retired, presence does not re-admit.
- **Freezing `Approved` forever, as Spine AD-5 states.** Keeping `Approved` and `PostingFailed` without any clock or human exit was rejected: both now carry a `PostingWindowElapsed` staleness bound, `Approved` accepts a human abandon by an Eligible Approver or the Tenant Agent Administrator, and the administrative retry has an age bound. The divergence from AD-5 is recorded in §8.1 and listed for Architecture.
- **A 30-day grace on every `DataHandlingVersion` change.** Rejected for changes that loosen protection, which now block Provider use immediately until the Tenant Agent Administrator accepts the new version; the grace remains only for changes that tighten protection, and "the version in force" is defined as the last accepted version (FR-4, OQ-29, A-10).
- **Host-supplied committed engine identity.** Comparing the attested engine against a value the same host composition supplies was rejected; the committed identity and version reach the runtime as a Release-Operator-owned `EXT-SECRETS-1` value, and the attestation records which principal supplied each compared value (FR-34).
- **Aggregation-free compliance inspection.** Leaving post-hoc inspections unbounded per Inspector was rejected; inspecting more than 5 distinct Conversations in 30 days is one wide inspection needing Platform Operator approval, an unreviewed inspection blocks the Inspector's next one and is a compliance finding, and the threshold values are provisional (A-23).
- **Keeping the FR-8 order with the classifier and reservation ahead of two local rejections.** Rejected; the steps were reordered so that the Conversation Agent State, rate limits, and Eligible Approver resolution precede context measurement, reservation, and the safety scan, and every step citation in the PRD was renumbered (FR-16, FR-32, SM-C4, §8.1).
- **Retrying a refused block mirror until it succeeds.** Rejected; only transient failures are retried, and a typed permanent refusal ends as `MirrorRefused`, surfaced to the Tenant Agent Administrator, with the block still in force (FR-2, OQ-25).
- **Protecting Agent Instructions under an Agent-level key.** Rejected for now so that `hexa` can be configured before the protection engine is `Available`; instructions stay unprotected but are erased at tenant offboarding, and the question is held open as OQ-31 with a Governance owner.
- **Dropping the Conversation status entry from FR-13.** Rejected; the entry becomes a required `EXT-CONV-UI-1` artifact kind, and the register extension is listed for correct-course.
- **Organization Parties as Approvers.** Rejected; every Approver Policy source must resolve to person Parties, and an organization Party source is a typed configuration rejection (FR-7, §6.2).
- **Storing unsafe human edits unscanned until approval.** Rejected; edits are scanned at edit time, an always-blocked failure is not stored, and a restricted failure is stored with a marker that makes the version unapprovable (FR-15, FR-27). The marker was named `SafetyFailed` here and renamed `RestrictedContent`, a version-record field, at this update's reviewer gate (below).
- **Keeping "the more restrictive of two policies".** Rejected as not computable; every re-check now requires the content to pass every applicable policy version, the same effect stated in a testable form (FR-26, OQ-9).
- **The `MessageId` lookup from `Approved`.** Performing the seam-2 existence read before the `Approved → Abandoned` transition, as Spine AD-5 does, was rejected: no post was attempted from `Approved`, so the lookup is skipped and a detected removal abandons directly (FR-18).
- **Blocking every reference to a Party's decision under FR-31.** Rejected because reporting a recorded position is what UJ-2 exists for; the policy now blocks first-person impersonation and decisions the Conversation does not record (FR-31, OQ-9).
- **Requiring n ≥ 300 for every p99 gate.** Rejected; `RQ-1` gates on p95 at 30 or more executions, and the p99 targets become gates only once a gate has 300 executions (NFR-9, NFR-14, OQ-5).
- **A PRD-side transitional acknowledgement that `ContextUnavailable` absorbs authorization denials.** Rejected; FR-9 defines `ContextUnavailable` as a context-read failure distinct from an authorization denial, and the code split is listed for correct-course.
- **Code-side rejection of an equal caller-supplied pricing version.** Rejected in favor of a server-assigned pricing version; a caller-supplied version is never stored as given, and a stale expected version is a typed rejection (FR-4).
- **A flat 20-call minimum sample below 5 calling Parties.** Rejected for the discontinuity it produced at 5 Parties; the sample is 10 calls per calling Party, bounded to 20–50 (FR-28, A-17).
- **Carrying the deferred lows again.** Rejected; the three low-severity findings drafted for carrying landed (UJ-1 wording, the SM-3 expiry basis, and the recorded Security approval on policy publication).
- **Undated FR-33 confirmations.** Rejected; the Product confirmations behind A-9 through A-12 carry a provisional due date of 2026-09-30 (A-26), and register-conditioned assumptions cite the register's `TargetIntegrationDate`.
- **Keeping the register gate rules inside FR-21.** Rejected; the process gates moved to §8, leaving FR-21 a one-line pointer to them.
- **Choosing the Story 5.3 non-conformance branch in the PRD.** Rejected; the PRD states only that an open Product approval blocks `ready-for-dev`, and Branch A or B is selected in the register.

- **Renumbering FR-8 to add a kill-switch step (H-7).** Rejected; the `Suspended` check and the shipped `CallerPartyState`, `DependencyFreshness`, `LaunchReadiness`, and policy-presence checks are folded into steps 1, 2, 4, and 9, so every existing step citation stays valid.

- **Computing the loosen-versus-tighten direction of a `DataHandlingVersion` change from typed fields (H-5).** Rejected; a change is tightening only when the Platform Operator declares it so with the recorded field-level diff, and every undeclared or loosening change blocks until accepted.

- **Ending the post-clear late-mirror branch on the first post-clear read that finds `hexa` present (C-1).** Rejected; a clear issues its own seam-1 add as a mirror entry, and the branch ends when that mirror is confirmed.
- **Escalating `TriggerReviewOverdue` to the Platform Operator after three business days (H-8).** Rejected; the condition blocks production-like enablement and activation until the review is recorded.
- **Bounding a `DeferredAssumption` revisit date by the first launch-health review (H-1).** Rejected; the bound is 30 calendar days, the deferral needs Product's acceptance, the evaluation scheduling is a recorded event, and Security-owned rows are never deferrable.
- **Counting `MembershipRejected` at acceptance as an unavailable call (M-5).** Rejected; it is reported separately (CI-9).
- **Counting a lapsed `DataHandlingVersion` acceptance as a blocked call (R-M-3, CI-2).** Rejected; it is its own SM-C4 class, excluded from the trigger shares because the tenant controls it.
- **Setting literal dates for the Architecture-owned `A-n` rows in the PRD (R-M-1).** Rejected; the PRD states the rule and the co-owners record the dates, while the Product-owned rows take the A-26 date.
- **Naming the edit-time marker `SafetyFailed` (D-M-2).** Rejected; the marker is the version-record field `RestrictedContent`, distinct from the call status.

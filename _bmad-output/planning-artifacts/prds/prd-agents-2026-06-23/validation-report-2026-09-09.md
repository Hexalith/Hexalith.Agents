**[Adversarial]** — C-2. The FR-2 membership step cannot re-admit `hexa` after a cleared block: its removal branch matches first, and the mirror's failure mode is undefined (§ FR-2 lines 148, 152–153; FR-33 row line 417 ("Remove, block, and re-admit"); OQ-16 line 872)

**[Adversarial]** — H-2. The kill-switch trigger is gameable by one hostile Participant and has no minimum sample (§ FR-28 line 622; SM-C4 line 846; FR-32 line 635; FR-33 row line 422)
**Quoted:** "a blocked-call share (SM-C4) above 50% or a posting-failure rate above 10% sustained for seven days, on which the Release Operator pulls it". **Attack:** SM-C4 is a share of *Agent Calls*, per tenant, with no minimum sample, no distinct-Party or distinct-Conversation denominator, and no exclusion of calls that were rejected cheaply. Blocked calls are the cheapest outcome in the system: an oversized Conversation rejects at FR-8 step 5 in under two seconds and spends nothing. Rate limits are per Party *and* per Conversation, so one Participant with access to several oversized Conversations (or one Conversation the Participant deliberately inflates past the Safe Context Budget) generates blocked calls at the rate-limit ceiling for seven days; in a low-traffic tenant that is far above 50%.

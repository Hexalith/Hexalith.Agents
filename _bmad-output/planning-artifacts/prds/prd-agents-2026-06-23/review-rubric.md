## Overall verdict

**The strongest revision of this PRD, and the first this month whose cross-document claims survive a check.** Every one of the baseline's five deferred decidability mediums has landed as a testable rule (a "pass every applicable policy version" safety rule, a public Agent Call contract with a terminal set, a p95-at-30 / p99-at-300 gate, a narrowed FR-31, one `ExpiredWhileSuspended`), §0 and §8 now say what the register says, and §8.1 names the one live Spine divergence instead of three dead ones. What is at risk is narrower and more product-shaped than before: UJ-2's tenant — Automatic Response Mode — is in scope on paper but blocked on a Product decision (OQ-23) whose revisit trigger cannot supply what it waits for; an automatic post that fails to post in a live Conversation has no human exit; two of the counter-metric's classification rules contradict each other on a lapsed data-handling acceptance; and one FR-24 threshold is a placeholder with no number. None of these blocks story extraction from the rest of the document.

Decisions are decisions. §13 holds 31 rows, each with an owner, a status, and a date; the four *Deferred* rows (OQ-15, OQ-18, OQ-23, OQ-31) each state a revisit condition, and the addendum records what was rejected and why for every 2026-09-09 decision.
